# Binding future V3 decisions

## Manual trade entry

- Manual trade entry is a required future V3 capability.
- It will use the future canonical execution and correction contracts.
- Every manual entry must retain user-entered provenance.
- User-entered data must never be represented as broker-confirmed evidence.
- Paper trades, owner-entered trades, broker-confirmed executions, and corrections must remain distinguishable.
- No manual-entry code is ported into active V3 runtime by this preservation task.

## AI period reflections

- AI period reflections are a required future V3 capability.
- The future capability includes daily, weekly, monthly, custom date-range, before/after-rule, and comparison-period reflections.
- Deterministic analytics and evidence calculate the facts.
- AI may later explain validated results; it may not become factual or financial authority.
- Mocked/local AI behavior, prompts, evidence contracts, usage reporting, and UI concepts are preserved here.
- No live model call is allowed or made by this task.
- V2 model output is not promoted to V3 factual authority.

## Real Coach / Whop

- Real Coach / Whop is a required future product capability.
- Human-coach sharing and Whop entitlement/payment are separate integrations.
- Human-coach sharing must later use owner-authorized evidence packages.
- Whop may control entitlement or commercial access only.
- Whop must never become the authority for financial calculations or journal data.
- Synthetic entitlement, access-expiry, idempotency, and fail-closed test concepts are preserved.
- No Whop call, real credential, payment, or runtime integration is performed by this task.
- The prototype's local role switch and demo identity assumptions are not production identity or authorization.

## Cross-cutting boundary

All three features require selective redesign against the controlling V3 contracts. The preserved code is evidence and reconstruction material, not an instruction to overwrite current main. GA0-A1 loopback, origin, deployment-profile, storage-path, route-containment, private-data, architecture, cache, and explicit-test-evidence controls remain authoritative.
