# Trader Intelligence v3 Legacy Hazard Register

Date: 2026-07-17
Gate: GA0-A1 containment and architecture boundaries

## Purpose

This register prevents contained prototype behavior from being mistaken for accepted v3 authority. `Temporary containment` means only that the current single-owner risk is bounded enough for further private development. It does not close the hazard.

## Release interpretation

GA0-A1 may be accepted only as a containment gate. H-004, H-014, H-015, H-016, and the A1 portion of H-018 have concrete controls in this branch. All other hazards remain open and block any claim that the prototype is production-ready. Private-hosted deployment also remains unauthorized until its real persistence, operational, and deployment prerequisites are implemented and reviewed.
