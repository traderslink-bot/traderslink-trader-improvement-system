## Voice Direction

The user specifically asked that the content should not sound too robotic or overly AI-generated.

The writing prompt was updated to push a more natural trading-community voice while still staying professional and careful.

Use plain English and grounded trader phrasing. The content should feel like practical trader education, not generic encyclopedia copy.

Good tone examples:

- "This is where traders can get into trouble."
- "The headline is only the start."
- "The chart can look exciting, but the filing details still matter."
- "This is not about predicting the move. It is about understanding the risk."
- "That sounds simple, but it matters."

Do not overdo personality. Keep it useful, practical, and professional.

## Compliance And Safety Guardrails

Continue avoiding:

- Financial advice
- Buy or sell signals
- Profit promises
- Claims that a chart pattern guarantees a move
- Claims that a filing guarantees dilution or a price move
- Claims that Trader Intelligence guarantees better results
- Claims that Trader Intelligence tells users what to trade

Use careful language:

- helps review
- helps organize
- helps analyze
- can help surface
- may indicate
- traders often watch
- context matters
- educational only
- not financial advice
