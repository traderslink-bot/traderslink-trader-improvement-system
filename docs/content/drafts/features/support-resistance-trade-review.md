# Support and Resistance Trade Review

Support and resistance trade review helps traders study whether their entries, exits, adds, and reductions happened near important price areas. Trader Intelligence is being built to support trade review with market context when support and resistance levels are available.

This feature is not about telling traders where to buy or sell. It is about helping traders review how their decisions interacted with price levels after the trade is complete.

## What This Feature Reviews

Support and resistance based review focuses on the relationship between a trader's executions and nearby price levels.

That can include:

- Entries near support.
- Entries into resistance.
- Exits near support.
- Exits into resistance.
- Adds after a level breaks.
- Adds into a weakening area.
- Reductions after rejection.
- Re-entries after reclaim attempts.
- Trade management around important levels.

The goal is to give traders more context when reviewing whether the trade was managed around meaningful areas on the chart.

## Why It Matters For Trade Review

Many trade reviews are incomplete without price context.

A trader may say, "I had a bad entry," but the better question is often more specific. Was the entry late into resistance? Was it close to support? Did the trade fail after rejecting a key level? Did the trader sell into support before a bounce? Did the trader add after the move was already extended?

Support and resistance context can help turn a vague review into a clearer one.

## How Trader Intelligence Uses This Information

Trader Intelligence is being built to review executions first. When market context and chart levels are available, the system can use that context to support better review.

That means support and resistance can be used as review context for:

- Entry quality.
- Exit quality.
- Add and reduction behavior.
- Trade management decisions.
- Failed breakout review.
- Reclaim and rejection review.
- Profit protection review.

This does not mean the app predicts levels or guarantees that a level will hold. Levels are context, not certainty.

## Example Review Questions

Support and resistance review can help traders ask:

- Did I buy directly under resistance?
- Did I sell into support without reviewing the chart context?
- Did I add after the stock was already extended from support?
- Did I reduce when price rejected a key area?
- Did I keep holding after the trade failed to reclaim a level?
- Did I chase a breakout instead of waiting for confirmation?
- Did my best trades start closer to support or after a reclaim?

These questions help traders review trade location and decision quality.

## Common Mistakes This Can Help Surface

Support and resistance based review can help surface issues such as:

- Buying too close to resistance.
- Selling too close to support.
- Adding after failed breakouts.
- Ignoring repeated rejection areas.
- Chasing a move away from the nearest support area.
- Holding after a breakdown through an important level.
- Re-entering before price has reclaimed a meaningful area.

The point is not that every level matters equally. The point is to help traders review whether price context affected their decisions.

## FAQ

### What is support and resistance trade review?

Support and resistance trade review is the process of studying how trade decisions happened around important price levels. It can help traders review whether they entered, exited, added, or reduced near meaningful chart areas.

### Does support and resistance guarantee a trade will work?

No. Support and resistance levels are context, not guarantees. A level can break, fail, reclaim, or become less relevant as price action changes.

### How can support and resistance help a trading journal?

Support and resistance can make trade review more specific. Instead of simply saying an entry was good or bad, a trader can review whether the entry was near support, under resistance, after a breakout, or after a rejection.

### Will Trader Intelligence use chart levels?

Trader Intelligence is being built to use market context when available. Support and resistance based review is planned as part of the broader trade review workflow.

### Is this a buy or sell signal feature?

No. This feature is for reviewing trades after the fact. It does not tell traders what to buy or sell.

### Can this help review failed breakouts?

Yes, support and resistance review can help traders study whether a trade failed after a breakout, rejection, failed reclaim, or breakdown through an important area.
