# Trading Mistake Tracker

A trading mistake tracker helps traders identify repeated behavior that may be hurting their process. Trader Intelligence is a coming trading mistake tracker from TradersLink designed to help active traders review execution problems, trade management issues, and repeated patterns across their trades.

The goal is not to shame a trader for a bad trade. The goal is to make repeated behavior easier to see and review.

## What This Feature Reviews

The trading mistake tracker is designed to help organize review around behaviors that show up across trades.

That can include:

- Chasing entries.
- Late entries.
- Poor exits.
- Adverse adds.
- Averaging down into weakness.
- Re-entering too many times.
- Cutting winners too early.
- Holding failed ideas too long.
- Weak profit protection.
- Repeated session timing problems.

A single mistake may not tell the full story. Repeated mistakes are often more important because they can show a pattern in the trader's decision-making.

## Why It Matters For Trade Review

Many traders know they make mistakes, but they do not always know which mistakes repeat most often.

After a fast trading day, it can be easy to remember the biggest win or the worst loss. It is harder to remember the smaller habits that show up again and again. Those habits can include entering too late, adding at the wrong time, failing to reduce risk, or exiting without a clear plan.

A trading mistake tracker helps turn those observations into a more structured review process.

## How Trader Intelligence Uses This Information

Trader Intelligence is being built to review broker executions and trade behavior so repeated issues can be surfaced more clearly.

The system is designed to look at what happened inside the trade, such as entries, exits, adds, reductions, timing, and trade management. From there, it can help organize possible mistake patterns for the trader to review.

This is review support, not financial advice. The app does not tell traders what to buy or sell. It helps traders study their own behavior after trades are taken.

## Example Review Questions

A trading mistake tracker can help traders ask questions like:

- Did I chase the move after the clean entry was already gone?
- Did I add to a losing trade without a strong reason?
- Did I cut the winner before the trade had enough room?
- Did I keep holding after the trade idea changed?
- Did I re-enter because there was a real setup or because I wanted the trade back?
- Did I trade worse at the open, midday, or after a loss?
- Is this mistake showing up across multiple trades?

These questions help shift the review away from emotion and toward process.

## Common Mistakes This Can Help Surface

Trader Intelligence is planned to support review around mistake categories such as:

- Entry quality problems.
- Exit quality problems.
- Scaling mistakes.
- Average down behavior.
- Profit giveback.
- Over-management.
- Poor risk reduction.
- Re-entry issues.
- Session timing problems.

The most useful part is not simply naming a mistake. The useful part is seeing whether the same behavior keeps appearing across different trades.

## FAQ

### What is a trading mistake tracker?

A trading mistake tracker is a tool or workflow that helps traders record and review repeated mistakes, such as chasing, poor exits, averaging down, overtrading, or weak trade management.

### Why should traders track mistakes?

Tracking mistakes helps traders see patterns that may not be obvious from one trade alone. Repeated behavior is often more useful to review than a single isolated mistake.

### Does Trader Intelligence automatically fix trading mistakes?

No. Trader Intelligence does not fix mistakes for the trader. It is being built to help surface and organize behavior so traders can review it more clearly.

### Can mistake tracking help with revenge trading or overtrading?

A structured review process can help traders notice patterns around revenge trading, overtrading, re-entry behavior, and session timing. The trader still needs to decide how to adjust their rules.

### Is this the same as a trading journal?

Mistake tracking can be part of a trading journal. Trader Intelligence is being built as a trading journal and trade review system with mistake tracking as one of the core features.

### Does Trader Intelligence provide financial advice?

No. Trader Intelligence does not provide financial advice or buy and sell signals. It is designed to support trade review and trader improvement.
