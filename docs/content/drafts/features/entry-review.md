# Trade Entry Review

Trade entry review helps traders study where and why they entered a trade. Trader Intelligence is being built to help active traders review entries, late entries, chasing behavior, support and resistance context, and execution quality after the trade is complete.

A trade entry is not just a price. It is a decision made in a specific market context. Reviewing that decision can help traders understand whether the trade started with structure or emotion.

## What This Feature Reviews

Entry review focuses on the first decision that opened the trade.

It can help review:

- Initial entry price.
- Entry timing.
- Entry location on the chart.
- Whether the entry was extended.
- Whether the entry happened near support or resistance.
- Whether the entry followed a plan or reacted to momentum.
- How price behaved after the entry.
- Whether similar entry mistakes repeat over time.

The goal is to help traders review the quality of the entry process, not simply label an entry as good or bad.

## Why It Matters For Trade Review

A poor entry can make the rest of the trade harder to manage.

If a trader enters too late, the trade may have less room before resistance. If a trader enters directly into weakness, the position may require quick risk control. If a trader chases after a large move, even a correct idea can become difficult to manage.

Entry review helps traders ask whether the trade started from a controlled decision or from a reaction.

## How Trader Intelligence Uses This Information

Trader Intelligence is being built to use imported executions as the starting point for entry review.

The system can help organize where the first entry happened, how the trade developed afterward, and whether available market context supports a clearer review. When support and resistance context is available, entry review can also consider whether the trader bought near support, under resistance, after a breakout, or after the move was already extended.

This is review support. It does not tell traders what to buy or when to enter.

## Example Review Questions

Entry review can help traders ask:

- Was my entry close to the planned area?
- Did I enter after the move was already extended?
- Did I buy directly under resistance?
- Did I enter before the trade gave enough confirmation?
- Did I chase because of fear of missing out?
- Did price move in my favor after entry or immediately reject?
- Is this type of entry mistake repeating across trades?

These questions can help traders review entry behavior with more detail.

## Common Mistakes This Can Help Surface

Trade entry review can help surface patterns such as:

- Chasing momentum after the clean entry has passed.
- Buying into resistance.
- Entering before confirmation.
- Entering because of scanner activity without a plan.
- Taking trades far from a risk area.
- Repeating late entries after missing the first move.
- Increasing size on lower-quality entries.

The purpose is not to make every entry perfect. The purpose is to make entry behavior easier to study.

## FAQ

### What is trade entry review?

Trade entry review is the process of studying where, when, and why a trader entered a position. It helps traders review whether the entry was planned, late, extended, or supported by context.

### Why should traders review entries?

Entries affect risk, trade management, and emotional pressure. A late or extended entry can make a trade harder to manage even if the trade idea is reasonable.

### Can Trader Intelligence identify late entries?

Trader Intelligence is being built to support entry review using execution data and available market context. It may help surface late or extended entry behavior for the trader to review.

### Is entry review the same as a buy signal?

No. Entry review happens after the trade and helps traders study their own decisions. It is not a buy signal or financial advice.

### Can entry review help with chasing?

Yes. A structured review process can help traders notice when they repeatedly enter after a move is already extended or after the best entry area has passed.
