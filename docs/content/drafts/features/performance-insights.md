# Trading Performance Insights

Trading performance insights help traders understand what is happening beneath the final profit and loss number. Trader Intelligence is being built to help active traders review execution quality, repeated mistakes, session timing, trade management behavior, and performance patterns with more structure.

The goal is not to reduce trading review to one score. The goal is to help traders see which behaviors may be helping or hurting their process over time.

## What This Feature Reviews

Performance insights focus on patterns across trades, not only one trade at a time.

They can help review:

- Entry behavior.
- Exit behavior.
- Adds and reductions.
- Scaling decisions.
- Mistake frequency.
- Session timing patterns.
- Profit protection behavior.
- Average down behavior.
- Trade duration.
- Performance by trade type or context when available.

A single trade can teach something, but repeated patterns across many trades are often more useful.

## Why It Matters For Trade Review

Many traders already know their P&L, but they do not always know what created it.

A trader may be profitable overall while still giving back too much open profit. Another trader may be struggling because of one repeated behavior, such as late entries or adverse adds. Without performance insights, those patterns can be difficult to separate from the noise of daily results.

Performance review helps traders ask better questions about process.

## How Trader Intelligence Uses This Information

Trader Intelligence is being built to use imported executions and structured review data to support performance insights.

The system can organize trade behavior into review categories such as execution, timing, trade management, mistakes, and outcomes. When enough trade history is available, those categories can help traders study repeated strengths and weaknesses.

This is not financial advice. It is a review tool for understanding past trading behavior.

## Example Review Questions

Performance insights can help traders ask:

- Which mistakes appear most often in my trades?
- Do I perform better in certain sessions or time windows?
- Are my biggest losses connected to adds, exits, or entries?
- Do I give back open profit too often?
- Do I trade better when I reduce risk quickly?
- Are my losing trades caused by one repeated behavior?
- Are my green trades actually well-managed or just good outcomes?

These questions help traders review process instead of reacting only to P&L.

## Common Mistakes This Can Help Surface

Performance insights can help surface patterns such as:

- Strong entries but weak exits.
- Frequent late entries.
- Repeated adverse adds.
- Poor performance at specific times of day.
- Profit giveback after strong starts.
- Overtrading after losses.
- Better results when position size is smaller.
- Worse results when re-entering too often.

The purpose is not to create a perfect report card. The purpose is to help traders review what is actually repeating.

## FAQ

### What are trading performance insights?

Trading performance insights are analytics or review summaries that help traders understand patterns in their trades, such as execution behavior, timing, mistakes, and trade management.

### How are performance insights different from P&L?

P&L shows the financial result. Performance insights help explain the behavior and decisions behind the result.

### Can Trader Intelligence show repeated mistakes?

Trader Intelligence is being built to help surface repeated trading mistakes and behavior patterns from execution and review data.

### Can performance insights help with day trading review?

Yes. Performance insights can help day traders review session timing, execution quality, trade management, and repeated behavior across intraday trades.

### Does Trader Intelligence guarantee better performance?

No. Trader Intelligence does not guarantee trading results. It is designed to support structured review and trader improvement.
