# Trade Exit Review

Trade exit review helps traders study how and why they closed a position. Trader Intelligence is being built to help active traders review exits, partial exits, missed exits, early sells, late sells, and profit protection behavior after the trade is complete.

A trade exit is not only the end of a trade. It is one of the most important decisions in the full trade review process.

## What This Feature Reviews

Exit review focuses on the decisions that reduced or closed the position.

It can help review:

- Partial exits.
- Final exits.
- Exit timing.
- Exit location on the chart.
- Whether the exit protected profit.
- Whether the exit happened too early.
- Whether the trader held after the idea failed.
- Whether the exit matched the original trade plan.
- Whether similar exit mistakes repeat over time.

The goal is to help traders study why they exited, not only where the exit happened.

## Why It Matters For Trade Review

Many traders struggle more with exits than entries.

A trader might sell too early because they are afraid to lose open profit. Another trader might hold too long because they do not want to accept that the trade has changed. A third trader might exit without a clear plan and then re-enter emotionally.

Exit review helps traders study whether their exits were controlled, reactive, defensive, late, or aligned with the trade idea.

## How Trader Intelligence Uses This Information

Trader Intelligence is being built to use imported executions as the foundation for exit review.

The system can help organize partial exits, reductions, and final exits so the trader can review how the position was closed. When market context is available, exit review can also consider whether the trader sold near support, into resistance, after rejection, after a failed reclaim, or after the trade had already weakened.

This is not a sell signal tool. It is a post-trade review workflow.

## Example Review Questions

Exit review can help traders ask:

- Did I exit because the trade idea failed or because I felt uncomfortable?
- Did I sell too early before the trade had enough room?
- Did I hold too long after price started weakening?
- Did I protect open profit or give too much back?
- Did I exit into support without considering context?
- Did my final exit happen after several avoidable warning signs?
- Is this exit behavior repeating across trades?

These questions help traders review the decision behind the exit.

## Common Mistakes This Can Help Surface

Trade exit review can help surface patterns such as:

- Selling winners too early.
- Holding losers too long.
- Exiting without a plan.
- Failing to reduce risk after a failed setup.
- Giving back open profit.
- Panic selling into weakness.
- Re-entering immediately after an emotional exit.
- Ignoring support or resistance context.

The purpose is not to make every exit perfect. The purpose is to help traders recognize repeated exit behavior.

## FAQ

### What is trade exit review?

Trade exit review is the process of studying how and why a trader closed or reduced a position. It can include partial exits, final exits, timing, chart context, and whether the exit matched the trade plan.

### Why should traders review exits?

Exits affect risk, profit protection, and trade management. Reviewing exits can help traders notice if they repeatedly sell too early, hold too long, or exit without a clear reason.

### Can Trader Intelligence review partial exits?

Trader Intelligence is being built to use imported executions, which can support review of partial exits, reductions, and final exits when that data is available.

### Is exit review a sell signal?

No. Exit review happens after the trade. It is designed to help traders study their own decisions, not tell them when to sell.

### Can exit review help with profit giveback?

Yes. A structured review can help traders study whether they protected open profit or repeatedly gave back gains before exiting.
