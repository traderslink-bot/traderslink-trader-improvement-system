# Trader Intelligence

Trader Intelligence is a coming trade review and trader improvement system from TradersLink. It is being built to help active traders review executions, study entries and exits, track repeated mistakes, and understand trade management behavior with more structure than a basic journal.

The goal is simple: help traders review how they actually traded, not just whether a trade ended green or red.

## What This Tool Does

Trader Intelligence is designed to turn trading activity into a clearer review process.

Instead of treating a journal as a place to type notes after the fact, Trader Intelligence is focused on execution-based review. The planned system starts with broker execution imports and uses that data to help organize trades into reviewable decisions.

That means the app can be built around questions like:

- Where did the trade start?
- Where did the trader add or reduce?
- Was the exit early, defensive, late, or structured?
- Did the trader keep adding into a weakening position?
- Did the trader protect open profit?
- Did the trader repeat the same behavior across multiple trades?

## Why Active Traders Need Better Review

Many traders track P&L, but P&L alone does not explain execution quality.

A profitable trade can still include chasing, poor risk control, or weak trade management. A losing trade can still include a valid entry, a controlled exit, or a disciplined reduction. Without structured review, those differences can be hard to see.

Trader Intelligence is being built for traders who want to study the decision process behind the result.

## How Trader Intelligence Helps

Trader Intelligence is planned to help traders organize and review:

- Broker executions.
- Entries and exits.
- Adds and reductions.
- Scaling behavior.
- Trade timing.
- Session performance.
- Repeated trading mistakes.
- Support and resistance context when available.
- Trade management behavior.

The system is not meant to replace trader judgment. It is meant to give traders a better review workflow so they can study their own behavior with more evidence.

## Who It Is For

Trader Intelligence is being built for active traders who want more structure in their review process.

It is especially useful for:

- Day traders.
- Small-cap traders.
- Penny stock traders.
- Momentum traders.
- Traders who scale in and out.
- Traders who want to track repeated mistakes.
- Traders who want to review execution quality.
- Traders who are tired of judging every trade only by P&L.

## What This Is Not

Trader Intelligence is not a trade alert service.

It does not provide financial advice.

It does not tell traders what to buy or sell.

It does not guarantee trading results.

It is a review and improvement tool. The purpose is to help traders study decisions, not to make decisions for them.
