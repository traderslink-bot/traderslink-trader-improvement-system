# Trading Journal App for Day Traders

A trading journal app should do more than record whether a trade made or lost money. Active traders need a way to review what they actually did: where they entered, how they managed the position, where they added, where they reduced, how long they held, and whether the same mistakes keep showing up.

Trader Intelligence is a coming trading journal app from TradersLink built around trade review, execution analysis, mistake tracking, and performance insights. It is designed for active day traders and small-cap traders who want a more structured way to study their trades after the fact.

## What This Tool Does

Trader Intelligence is being built to help traders import broker executions and turn those executions into useful review data.

Instead of relying only on manual notes, the app is designed to organize trade activity into a clearer review workflow. The goal is to help traders study entries, exits, adds, reductions, timing, and trade management behavior.

This matters because many trading mistakes do not show up clearly in a basic profit and loss number. A green trade can still include poor execution. A red trade can still include a disciplined decision. A better journal should help separate outcome from process.

## Why Active Traders Need Better Review

Fast-moving traders often take multiple executions in one session. A single position may include an entry, partial exits, re-adds, trims, and a final exit. If those executions are reviewed only from memory, important details can get missed.

A better trading journal app should help answer questions like:

- Did I enter too late after the move was already extended?
- Did I add into weakness or add into strength?
- Did I reduce risk when the trade stopped working?
- Did I cut the winner too early or hold the loser too long?
- Did my best trades happen at a certain time of day?
- Did I repeat the same mistake across several trades?

Trader Intelligence is designed around those types of review questions.

## How Trader Intelligence Helps

Trader Intelligence focuses on structured trade review rather than simple note-taking.

The planned workflow is centered on imported executions, saved review data, and evidence-backed feedback. When market context is available, the system can also support review around support, resistance, session timing, and trade management behavior.

The purpose is not to tell traders what to buy or sell. The purpose is to help traders study their own decisions with more structure.

## FAQ

### What is a trading journal app?

A trading journal app is a tool that helps traders record and review their trades. A basic journal may track entries, exits, size, and P&L. A more advanced journal can help traders review execution, behavior, mistakes, timing, and trade management.

### How is Trader Intelligence different from a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet can track trade data, but it usually requires manual work. Trader Intelligence is being built to use imported executions and structured review data so traders can study behavior and execution more clearly.

### Is Trader Intelligence live yet?

Trader Intelligence is marked as coming soon. It is part of the TradersLink product roadmap for beta members.

### Does Trader Intelligence give buy or sell signals?

No. Trader Intelligence is not a buy or sell signal tool. It is designed for trade review, execution analysis, mistake tracking, and performance insights.

### Can I import broker executions?

Broker execution import is a planned part of Trader Intelligence. The goal is to make trade review more accurate than relying only on manual notes.

### Why is P&L alone not enough for trade review?

P&L tells you the result, but it does not always explain the quality of the decision. A trader can make money with poor process or lose money after following a valid plan. Trade review helps study the process behind the result.
