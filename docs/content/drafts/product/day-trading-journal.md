# Day Trading Journal for Active Traders

A day trading journal should help active traders review more than basic profit and loss. Intraday traders need to study entries, exits, adds, reductions, timing, trade management, and repeated mistakes across fast-moving sessions.

Trader Intelligence is a coming day trading journal from TradersLink built for structured trade review, execution analysis, mistake tracking, and performance insights.

## What This Tool Does

Trader Intelligence is being built to help day traders organize and review their trades with more structure.

The planned workflow focuses on imported broker executions and review data instead of only manual notes. That can help traders study what happened inside a trade, including when the trade started, how it was managed, and where it ended.

For day traders, this is important because a single session can include several trades, several partial exits, and multiple decisions made under pressure.

## Why Active Day Traders Need Better Review

Day trading moves quickly. It is easy to forget why a trade was taken, where the plan changed, or whether an exit was controlled or emotional.

A day trading journal should help answer questions like:

- Did I trade better in premarket, at the open, midday, or later in the day?
- Did I enter after the best move had already happened?
- Did I add into strength or into weakness?
- Did I protect open profit?
- Did I overtrade after a loss?
- Did I repeat the same mistake across multiple trades?

Without structured review, those questions often get reduced to one number: daily P&L. That is not enough for meaningful improvement.

## FAQ

### What is a day trading journal?

A day trading journal is a tool or workflow used to record and review intraday trades. It can include entries, exits, size, timing, notes, screenshots, mistakes, and performance data.

### Why do day traders need a journal?

Day traders make decisions quickly. A journal helps them slow down after the session and review whether their trades followed a plan, where mistakes repeated, and how execution affected results.

### How is Trader Intelligence different from a basic day trading journal?

Trader Intelligence is being built to focus on imported executions, trade review, execution analysis, mistake tracking, and performance insights instead of only manual notes.

### Can a day trading journal track time of day?

Yes. A strong journal can help review whether a trader performs differently in premarket, at the open, midday, afternoon, or after-hours sessions. Session time analysis is a planned Trader Intelligence feature.

### Does Trader Intelligence give day trading signals?

No. Trader Intelligence is not a signal service. It is designed to help traders review trades they already took.
