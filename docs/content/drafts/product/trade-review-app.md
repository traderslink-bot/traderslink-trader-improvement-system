# Trade Review App for Active Traders

A trade review app helps traders study what happened after a trade is complete. Trader Intelligence is a coming trade review app from TradersLink designed to help active traders analyze executions, entries, exits, adds, reductions, mistakes, and trade management behavior.

The goal is not to judge a trade only by the final profit or loss. The goal is to review the decisions that created the result.

## What This Tool Does

Trader Intelligence is being built as a structured trade review tool.

Instead of only saving notes, screenshots, and P&L, the app is planned to help traders review the actual sequence of decisions inside a trade. That can include the first entry, later adds, partial exits, risk reductions, final exits, session timing, and repeated behavior across multiple trades.

A strong review process can help traders see whether they followed a plan, reacted emotionally, managed risk, protected profit, or repeated a mistake.

## Why Active Traders Need Better Review

Many traders remember trades emotionally. A big winner can feel like a perfect trade. A loss can feel like a failure. But the real review is usually more detailed than that.

A trade can be profitable but still include poor habits. A trade can lose money but still show good risk control. A trade review app should help traders separate the outcome from the execution process.

For active traders, this matters because patterns often repeat:

- Entering late after a move is extended.
- Adding to a weakening position.
- Cutting a winner before the trade has enough room.
- Holding a loser after the original idea failed.
- Re-entering without a clear reason.
- Trading worse during certain parts of the session.

Trader Intelligence is designed to help surface those kinds of review points.

## How Trader Intelligence Helps

Trader Intelligence is planned to use imported broker executions and structured review logic to help traders organize trades into clearer decision points.

The app can support review questions like:

- Was the entry near a useful area or late into momentum?
- Did the trader reduce risk when the trade changed?
- Did adds improve the position or increase risk?
- Did the exit match the trade idea?
- Did the trader protect open profit?
- Did the same mistake repeat across multiple trades?

This is designed as review support, not financial advice or trade instruction.

## FAQ

### What is a trade review app?

A trade review app is a tool that helps traders analyze completed trades. It can help review entries, exits, position changes, mistakes, timing, and trade management behavior.

### How is a trade review app different from a trading journal?

A trading journal records trade information. A trade review app focuses more on analyzing the decisions inside the trade and helping the trader understand what happened.

### Is Trader Intelligence a trade review app?

Yes. Trader Intelligence is being built as a trading journal and trade review app for active traders.

### Does Trader Intelligence tell me what trades to take?

No. Trader Intelligence does not provide buy or sell signals. It is designed to help traders review trades they already took.

### Can a trade review app help with repeated mistakes?

A structured review process can help traders notice repeated behaviors, such as chasing, poor exits, adverse adds, or weak trade management. Trader Intelligence is being built with mistake tracking as a core feature.
