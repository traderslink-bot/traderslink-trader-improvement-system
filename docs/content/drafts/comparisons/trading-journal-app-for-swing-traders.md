# Trading Journal App For Swing Traders

A trading journal app for swing traders should help review multi-day trade ideas, entries, exits, risk, catalysts, overnight exposure, and trade management. Swing trades often unfold over several days, so the journal needs to capture more than a single entry and exit.

A useful journal should help swing traders understand whether the original thesis stayed valid, whether risk was managed, and whether exits were planned or emotional.

## What Swing Traders Need Most

Swing traders need a journal that can handle time, context, and changing conditions.

Useful journal features include:

1. Trade thesis notes.
2. Catalyst tracking.
3. Multi-day screenshots.
4. Entry and exit review.
5. Risk plan tracking.
6. Overnight risk notes.
7. Support and resistance context.
8. Trade management review.
9. Setup tags.
10. Performance by strategy.

The goal is to review whether the swing trade stayed aligned with the original idea.

## Why Swing Trading Needs Structured Review

Swing trades can change as new candles, news, filings, market conditions, and levels develop.

This is where traders can get into trouble. A trader may enter with a plan, then hold after the reason for the trade is no longer valid. Or they may exit early because of normal volatility and miss whether the original setup remained intact.

A stronger review asks:

1. What was the original thesis?
2. Was risk defined before entry?
3. Did the stock hold key levels?
4. Did the catalyst remain valid?
5. Did new filings or news change the risk?
6. Did the trader manage the position according to plan?
7. Was the exit based on structure or fear?
8. Did the trader hold through avoidable risk?

Swing trading review should connect the trade idea with management over time.

## Execution Review For Swing Traders

Execution matters in swing trading because the entry and exit can affect the whole risk profile.

A journal should help review:

1. Buying too extended.
2. Entering before confirmation.
3. Ignoring support failure.
4. Holding through thesis failure.
5. Cutting winners too early.
6. Adding without a plan.
7. Ignoring overnight risk.
8. Missing filing or dilution risk.

A swing trade can be a good idea at first and become a poor hold later if conditions change.

## Example Scenario

A trader enters a swing trade after a stock reclaims a key level. Two days later, the stock fails that level and new dilution risk appears in filings, but the trader continues holding.

A stronger journal review asks:

1. Was the original thesis still valid?
2. Did the failed level trigger a planned exit?
3. Did the new filing change risk?
4. Was the hold based on structure or hope?
5. Was position size appropriate for overnight risk?
6. Has the trader held past thesis failure before?

This kind of review can make swing trade lessons more specific.

## Common Mistakes

One common mistake is writing the entry thesis but never updating it during the trade.

Another mistake is treating normal pullbacks and thesis failure the same way.

Swing traders also get into trouble when they ignore overnight news, dilution filings, or market weakness.

A fourth mistake is not reviewing trade management decisions between entry and exit.

## FAQ

### What should swing traders track in a journal?

Swing traders should track thesis, catalyst, entry, stop, target, support and resistance, overnight risk, position management, screenshots, and exit reason.

### Why does thesis tracking matter for swing trades?

Swing trades can last multiple days, so the original thesis may change as new price action, filings, news, or market conditions develop.

### Should swing traders track filings and news?

Yes. Filings, offerings, earnings, and news can change the risk profile of a multi-day trade.

### Can a journal app help with overnight risk?

A journal app can help traders document and review overnight risk decisions, but it cannot remove that risk.

### How does Trader Intelligence fit swing trading review?

Trader Intelligence is being built to support structured trade review and execution analysis, which can help swing traders review thesis alignment and trade management.

### Does a journal app guarantee swing trading success?

No. A journal app does not guarantee success. Swing trading still requires risk management, discipline, and market awareness.
