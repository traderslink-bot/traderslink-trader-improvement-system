# VWAP Reclaim

A VWAP reclaim happens when price moves back above VWAP after trading below it. Traders often watch this because VWAP can act as an intraday reference area for average price, trend, and market participation.

A VWAP reclaim does not guarantee continuation. Price can reclaim VWAP, fail, and move back below it quickly. The reclaim matters only if the price action, volume, and context support the idea.

This is not about treating VWAP as a signal. It is about reviewing how price behaves around an important intraday level.

## What The Pattern Looks Like

A VWAP reclaim usually starts with price trading below VWAP.

The basic sequence may look like this:

- Price is below VWAP.
- Price pushes back toward VWAP.
- Price moves above VWAP.
- Traders watch whether VWAP holds as support.
- Price either continues higher or fails back below VWAP.

Some VWAP reclaims happen after a morning washout. Others happen after consolidation, news, or a failed breakdown.

The key is the reaction after the reclaim.

## Why Traders Watch It

Traders watch VWAP reclaim patterns because VWAP is widely followed by intraday traders.

If a stock has been weak below VWAP and then reclaims it with volume, some traders may view that as a possible shift in intraday control. If price then holds above VWAP, the reclaim may become more meaningful.

Traders often watch:

- Whether price reclaims VWAP with volume.
- Whether VWAP holds after reclaim.
- Whether the stock is near high of day.
- Whether the reclaim happens with a catalyst.
- Whether there is room before resistance.

VWAP is only one level. Support, resistance, volume, and news still matter.

## What Can Go Wrong

The biggest problem is a failed reclaim.

Price may move above VWAP, attract buyers, and then fall back below it. If that happens, the reclaim may become a trap for traders who entered too late or without a risk plan.

Other problems include:

- Reclaiming VWAP on weak volume.
- Reclaiming directly into resistance.
- Chasing after a big reclaim candle.
- Ignoring overall downtrend structure.
- Treating VWAP as exact instead of a zone.
- Holding after VWAP is lost again.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They see price above VWAP and assume the trade is safe. It is not.

## Volume And Context

Volume can help traders review whether the reclaim has participation.

A reclaim with stronger volume may show that more traders are participating in the shift. A reclaim on thin volume may be less convincing, especially if price is still below major resistance.

Context matters. A VWAP reclaim after strong news may behave differently from a reclaim in a stock with no catalyst. A reclaim near high of day may also be different from a reclaim below several resistance levels.

Traders should review:

- Catalyst quality.
- Relative volume.
- Support and resistance.
- Float and liquidity.
- Time of day.
- Whether VWAP holds after reclaim.

## Small-Cap Considerations

Small-cap VWAP reclaims can move quickly and fail quickly.

A low-float stock may reclaim VWAP, spike, halt, reject, or flush in a short period. Wide spreads and thin liquidity can make entries and exits harder.

Small-cap traders should review:

- Whether the reclaim has real volume.
- Whether the spread is manageable.
- Whether there is a news catalyst.
- Whether the stock has already made a large move.
- Whether the reclaim is into resistance.
- Whether VWAP is being respected or chopped through repeatedly.

A VWAP reclaim in a clean trend is different from a VWAP reclaim in a choppy stock.

## How To Review Trades Involving This Pattern

A VWAP reclaim trade review should focus on reclaim quality and risk control.

Useful journal questions include:

- Was price clearly below VWAP before reclaiming it?
- Did volume support the reclaim?
- Did I enter before or after VWAP reclaimed?
- Did VWAP hold after entry?
- Was there nearby resistance?
- Where was the invalidation level?
- Did I chase after the reclaim candle?
- Did I exit if price lost VWAP again?

These questions help traders separate a planned reclaim trade from a FOMO entry above a popular level.

## FAQ

### What is a VWAP reclaim?

A VWAP reclaim happens when price moves back above VWAP after previously trading below it.

### Is VWAP reclaim bullish?

It can be viewed as constructive by some traders, but it does not guarantee continuation. Price can reclaim VWAP and fail quickly.

### What confirms a VWAP reclaim?

Many traders watch whether price reclaims VWAP with volume and then holds above it instead of falling back below.

### Can VWAP reclaim trades fail?

Yes. Price can move above VWAP, reject, and lose the level again.

### Why does volume matter with VWAP reclaim?

Volume can help show whether the reclaim has participation, but it does not guarantee follow-through.

### How should I journal VWAP reclaim trades?

Track the reclaim level, entry timing, volume, whether VWAP held, nearby resistance, invalidation level, and whether you followed the plan.
