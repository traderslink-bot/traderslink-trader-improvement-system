# Trading Discipline

Trading discipline is the ability to follow a trading plan, risk rules, and review process even when emotions are high. It includes waiting for setups, respecting stops, avoiding impulsive trades, and managing risk consistently.

Trading discipline does not mean every trade will work. It means the trader is trying to follow a repeatable process instead of reacting emotionally.

## Simple Definition

Trading discipline means following the plan.

Trading discipline may include:

1. Waiting for the setup.
2. Using correct position size.
3. Respecting stops.
4. Avoiding revenge trading.
5. Avoiding FOMO entries.
6. Stopping after max loss.
7. Reviewing trades honestly.
8. Following written rules.

The goal is to reduce avoidable mistakes.

## Why Trading Discipline Matters

Trading discipline matters because emotional decisions can damage otherwise reasonable trade ideas.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may know the rule but break it in real time because of fear, frustration, greed, or pressure.

A useful review asks:

1. Did the trader follow the plan?
2. Did emotion change the decision?
3. Was the entry disciplined?
4. Was the exit disciplined?
5. Were risk rules followed?
6. What discipline issue repeated?

Trading discipline should be reviewed through actual trade behavior.

## Example

A trader plans to stop trading after two losses. After the second loss, they take another unplanned trade to make the money back. That is a discipline issue, even if the third trade happens to win.

The review should focus on the rule break, not only the result.

## How To Review Trading Discipline

A good discipline review compares rules with actions.

Useful questions include:

1. What rule was in place?
2. Was the rule followed?
3. What emotion appeared?
4. Did the trader pause before acting?
5. Did the journal capture the mistake?
6. What rule can prevent it next time?

The goal is to find practical ways to reduce repeated emotional decisions.

## FAQ

### What is trading discipline?

Trading discipline is the ability to follow a trading plan, rules, and risk limits.

### Why is discipline important in trading?

It helps reduce emotional decisions, impulsive trades, and avoidable rule breaks.

### Does discipline mean never losing?

No. Disciplined traders still lose trades, but they aim to follow their process.

### How can traders review discipline?

They can compare planned rules with actual trade behavior and look for repeated mistakes.

### What are common discipline problems?

Common issues include overtrading, revenge trading, FOMO entries, moving stops, and oversized positions.
