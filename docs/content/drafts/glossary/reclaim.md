# Reclaim

A reclaim happens when price moves back above a key level after previously losing it. Traders often watch reclaims because they can show that price is recovering a level that had briefly failed.

A reclaim does not guarantee continuation. Price can reclaim a level and still reject, chop, or fail again. Traders need to review confirmation, volume, and risk.

## Simple Definition

A reclaim is when price gets back above a level it previously lost.

A reclaim may involve:

1. Support that was lost and then regained.
2. A prior breakout level.
3. High of day.
4. Previous day high.
5. VWAP or moving average.
6. A psychological level.
7. A breakdown level.
8. A failed fakeout area.

The important part is that price had lost the level first, then moved back above it.

## Why Reclaims Matter

Reclaims matter because they can show a shift after a failed breakdown or failed weakness.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may enter a reclaim too early before price actually holds the level, or they may ignore that the reclaim failed shortly after entry.

A useful review asks:

1. What level was reclaimed?
2. Was the level meaningful?
3. Did price hold above it?
4. Did volume support the reclaim?
5. Was the entry planned or chased?
6. What invalidated the reclaim trade?

A reclaim should be reviewed for follow-through, not just the first move back above the level.

## Example

A stock breaks below $2.00 support, then quickly moves back above $2.00 and holds. That move back above $2.00 is a reclaim.

If the stock cannot hold above $2.00 and falls back below, the reclaim may have failed.

## How To Review A Reclaim

A good reclaim review looks at level quality and execution timing.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the reclaim level clear?
2. Did price confirm above the level?
3. Did the trader enter before or after confirmation?
4. Was risk defined below the reclaim area?
5. Did volume support the move?
6. Did the trader exit if the reclaim failed?

The goal is to understand whether the reclaim trade had structure or was just a reaction to a fast bounce.

## FAQ

### What is a reclaim in trading?

A reclaim happens when price moves back above a level it previously lost.

### Does a reclaim guarantee a reversal?

No. A reclaim can fail, reject, or continue depending on price action and context.

### What confirms a reclaim?

Confirmation may include price holding above the level, volume, a retest, or continued buying pressure.

### Is a reclaim the same as a breakout?

Not exactly. A breakout moves above resistance or a range. A reclaim regains a level that was previously lost.

### Why do traders review reclaim trades?

They review them to understand whether the level was meaningful and whether entry and risk were handled properly.
