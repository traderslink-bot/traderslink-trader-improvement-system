# Trading Plan

A trading plan is a structured set of rules and guidelines a trader uses to decide what to trade, when to trade, how much to risk, and when to stop. It helps turn trading from reactive decisions into a repeatable process.

A trading plan does not guarantee profits. It gives the trader a framework for preparation, execution, and review.

## Simple Definition

A trading plan is the overall plan for how a trader trades.

A trading plan may include:

1. Preferred setups.
2. Trade criteria.
3. Risk limits.
4. Position sizing rules.
5. Watchlist process.
6. Entry and exit rules.
7. Trade review process.
8. Rules for stopping after losses.

The goal is to make decisions less emotional and more consistent.

## Why A Trading Plan Matters

A trading plan matters because it gives the trader something to follow and review.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may say they have a plan, but the plan is not specific enough to guide real decisions.

A useful review asks:

1. Was there a written plan?
2. Were the setups clear?
3. Were risk limits defined?
4. Did the trader follow the plan?
5. Where did the plan fail?
6. What rule should be improved?

A trading plan should be specific enough to review after the fact.

## Example

A trader’s plan may allow only two setups, define a max daily loss, require a stop before entry, and require a post-trade review. If the trader takes random trades outside those rules, the review can flag the issue clearly.

Without a plan, it is harder to know what went wrong.

## How To Review A Trading Plan

A good trading plan review compares written rules with actual behavior.

Useful questions include:

1. Did the trader follow setup criteria?
2. Were risk rules respected?
3. Were exits consistent with the plan?
4. Did the trader overtrade?
5. Did the plan fit current market conditions?
6. What needs to be clarified?

The goal is to improve the plan and the trader’s ability to follow it.

## FAQ

### What is a trading plan?

A trading plan is a structured set of rules and guidelines for how a trader prepares, enters, manages, exits, and reviews trades.

### Is a trading plan the same as a trade plan?

A trading plan is the broader system. A trade plan is usually the plan for one specific trade.

### What should a trading plan include?

It can include setups, criteria, position sizing, risk limits, entry rules, exit rules, watchlist process, and review process.

### Does a trading plan guarantee profitability?

No. A trading plan does not guarantee profit, but it can help make decisions more consistent and reviewable.

### How can traders review a trading plan?

They can compare the plan with actual trades and identify which rules were followed or broken.
