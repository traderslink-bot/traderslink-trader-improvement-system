# Forward Testing

Forward testing means testing a strategy, setup, or rule set in current market conditions going forward. Traders may forward test with paper trading, simulated trades, very small size, or observation before committing more risk.

Forward testing does not guarantee future profitability. It helps traders see how a strategy behaves outside historical testing.

## Simple Definition

Forward testing is testing a trading idea as new market data appears.

Forward testing may involve:

1. Paper trades.
2. Simulator trades.
3. Small-size live trades.
4. Watchlist tracking.
5. Alert tracking.
6. Setup journaling.
7. Rule validation.
8. Execution review.
9. Risk review.
10. Performance review.

The goal is to test whether the idea works in real-time conditions.

## Why Forward Testing Matters

Forward testing matters because backtests can look better than live trading.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may trust a historical test without seeing whether the setup is practical in the current market.

A useful review asks:

1. Were the rules followed exactly?
2. Did current market conditions support the setup?
3. Were fills realistic?
4. Did the trader hesitate or chase?
5. Did the strategy still work outside the backtest?
6. Should risk remain small?

Forward testing helps bridge research and live trading.

## Example

A trader backtests a pullback strategy and then forward tests it for one month using paper trades. Each time the setup appears, the trader records the planned entry, stop, target, and result.

This helps test whether the strategy is practical in current conditions.

## How To Review Forward Testing

A good forward testing review looks at live decision quality.

Useful questions include:

1. Was each setup recorded in real time?
2. Were rules followed without hindsight?
3. Did execution assumptions hold up?
4. Did the trader skip valid setups?
5. Did the trader take invalid setups?
6. Is the sample large enough to continue testing?

The goal is to decide whether the strategy deserves more attention, adjustment, or rejection.

## FAQ

### What is forward testing?

Forward testing is testing a trading idea in current market conditions as new data appears.

### How is forward testing different from backtesting?

Backtesting uses historical data. Forward testing evaluates the strategy going forward in real-time or simulated real-time conditions.

### Can forward testing use paper trading?

Yes. Paper trading is one way to forward test a setup without real money at risk.

### Does forward testing guarantee a strategy will work live?

No. Forward testing does not guarantee live results, especially if real money changes behavior or fills.

### Why should traders journal forward tests?

Journaling helps keep the test honest and makes rule-following easier to review.
