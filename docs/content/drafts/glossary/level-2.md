# Level 2

Level 2 is a market data display that shows bid and ask depth beyond the best bid and ask. Traders use Level 2 to review market depth, liquidity, spread, and visible buying or selling interest at different prices.

Level 2 does not guarantee future price movement. Orders can change quickly, and displayed size may not show the full market.

## Simple Definition

Level 2 shows market depth by price level.

It may show:

1. Bid prices.
2. Ask prices.
3. Bid size.
4. Ask size.
5. Market makers or venues.
6. Spread.
7. Depth at multiple levels.
8. Liquidity changes.

The exact display depends on the platform, data feed, and exchange coverage.

## Why Level 2 Matters

Level 2 matters because it can help traders review execution conditions before entering or exiting a trade.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may see a large bid or ask and assume it will stay there, but liquidity can disappear quickly.

A useful review asks:

1. What did Level 2 show before entry?
2. Was the spread wide?
3. Was there enough size at the bid or ask?
4. Did visible liquidity change quickly?
5. Did the trader get slippage?
6. Did Level 2 support or conflict with the trade plan?

Level 2 is an execution tool, not a guarantee.

## Example

A stock shows a best bid at $2.00 and a best ask at $2.05. Level 2 may also show bids below $2.00 and asks above $2.05, giving the trader a broader view of market depth.

If depth is thin, a trader may need to be more careful with size and order type.

## How To Review Level 2

A good Level 2 review looks at liquidity, spread, and execution quality.

Useful questions include:

1. Did Level 2 show enough depth?
2. Did the spread widen during the trade?
3. Did the bid support price?
4. Did the ask lift quickly?
5. Did visible size disappear?
6. Did the trader rely too heavily on displayed orders?

The goal is to understand whether Level 2 conditions affected the trade.

## FAQ

### What is Level 2 in trading?

Level 2 is a market data display that shows bid and ask depth beyond the best bid and ask.

### Is Level 2 the same as the order book?

Level 2 is one way traders view order book depth, depending on the data feed and platform.

### Does Level 2 predict price direction?

No. Level 2 can help review liquidity and execution context, but it does not guarantee direction.

### Why do traders watch Level 2?

They watch it to understand spread, visible liquidity, market depth, and possible execution risk.

### Can Level 2 be misleading?

Yes. Orders can be cancelled, moved, hidden, or changed quickly.
