# Drawdown

Drawdown is the decline from a prior account high to a lower point. Traders use drawdown to understand how much their account, strategy, or performance has pulled back from a peak.

Drawdown is not just about losing trades. It can show whether risk is controlled, whether losses are clustering, and whether the trader is following rules during difficult periods.

## Simple Definition

Drawdown measures how far performance falls from a previous high.

For example:

1. Account reaches $10,000.
2. Account drops to $9,000.
3. Drawdown is $1,000, or 10%.

That drawdown continues until the account makes a new high.

## Why Drawdown Matters

Drawdown matters because every trader will face losing periods. The question is whether the losses are controlled and reviewable.

This is where traders can get into trouble. A small drawdown can become much worse if the trader revenge trades, oversizes, or abandons the plan.

A useful review asks:

1. How large is the drawdown?
2. What caused it?
3. Was it from one big loss or many small losses?
4. Did the trader follow daily risk limits?
5. Did the trader overtrade during the drawdown?
6. Did the strategy conditions change?

Drawdown review is about understanding risk behavior during pressure.

## Example

A trader starts the month strong and reaches a new account high. Over the next week, they lose money for four sessions in a row. The account is now down from its high.

A strong review asks whether the drawdown came from normal strategy variance or from broken rules, oversizing, chasing, or emotional trading.

## How To Review Drawdown

A good drawdown review looks at the cause, size, and behavior during the decline.

Useful questions include:

1. When did the drawdown start?
2. Which trades caused most of it?
3. Were losses planned or avoidable?
4. Did trade quality change?
5. Did risk increase after losses?
6. What rule should be reinforced?

The goal is to reduce avoidable damage, not to pretend losses never happen.

## FAQ

### What is drawdown in trading?

Drawdown is the decline from a prior account or performance high to a lower point.

### Is drawdown the same as a loss?

Not exactly. A loss is one result. Drawdown measures decline from a peak over a period of performance.

### Why do traders track drawdown?

They track it to understand risk, consistency, emotional pressure, and how much damage occurs during losing periods.

### What causes drawdown?

Drawdown can come from normal losing trades, poor execution, oversizing, overtrading, changing market conditions, or rule breaks.

### Is some drawdown normal?

Yes. Drawdowns are part of trading. The key is whether they are controlled and reviewed.
