# Debt

Debt is money a company owes to lenders, noteholders, or other creditors. Traders review debt because it can affect cash runway, financing risk, dilution risk, and company stability.

Debt is not automatically bad. Some companies use debt to fund growth or operations. The key question is whether the debt is manageable and how it may affect shareholders.

## Simple Definition

Debt is a financial obligation a company must repay or service.

Debt may include:

1. Bank loans.
2. Notes payable.
3. Convertible notes.
4. Short-term debt.
5. Long-term debt.
6. Lines of credit.
7. Lease obligations.
8. Accrued interest.

The details usually appear in quarterly and annual filings.

## Why Debt Matters

Debt matters because repayment obligations can pressure cash and operations.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may review cash on hand but miss debt maturity dates or convertible debt terms.

A useful review asks:

1. How much debt does the company have?
2. Is it short-term or long-term?
3. What are the maturity dates?
4. What is the interest expense?
5. Is any debt convertible into shares?
6. Could debt create dilution risk?

Debt should be reviewed with cash, working capital, and cash runway.

## Example

A company has $15 million in cash but also has $20 million of short-term debt coming due. The cash balance alone does not tell the full story because near-term obligations may create financing pressure.

A trader should review the timing and terms of the debt.

## How To Review Debt

A good debt review looks at amount, timing, and terms.

Useful questions include:

1. How much debt is outstanding?
2. When does it mature?
3. Is the interest expense high?
4. Can debt convert into shares?
5. Are there default risks?
6. Did management discuss refinancing?

The goal is to understand whether debt changes the company risk profile.

## FAQ

### What is debt?

Debt is money a company owes to lenders, noteholders, or other creditors.

### Is debt always bad for a company?

No. Debt can fund growth or operations, but it can also create repayment pressure and risk.

### Why do traders review debt?

They review debt to understand cash needs, maturity risk, interest expense, and possible dilution from convertible securities.

### What is short-term debt?

Short-term debt is debt due within a shorter period, often within one year.

### Can debt cause dilution?

Convertible debt can create dilution if it converts into shares.
