# Operating Expenses

Operating expenses are the costs a company incurs to run its business. Traders review operating expenses because they can affect cash burn, net loss, cash runway, and financing risk.

Operating expenses are not automatically bad. Companies need to spend money to operate, develop products, hire staff, market services, and grow. The key question is whether expenses are sustainable relative to cash, revenue, and business progress.

## Simple Definition

Operating expenses are the regular costs of running a company.

Common operating expenses include:

1. Research and development.
2. Sales and marketing.
3. General and administrative expenses.
4. Salaries and benefits.
5. Rent and facilities.
6. Professional fees.
7. Insurance.
8. Technology and operating costs.

The categories can vary by company and industry.

## Why Operating Expenses Matter

Operating expenses matter because they help explain how quickly a company uses cash.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may focus on cash on hand without checking how quickly the company is spending that cash.

A useful review asks:

1. What are operating expenses?
2. Are expenses increasing or decreasing?
3. What is the cash burn rate?
4. Is revenue offsetting expenses?
5. Does the company have enough cash runway?
6. Could financing be needed?

Operating expense review should be connected to cash runway and dilution risk.

## Example

A company has $20 million in cash but spends $8 million per quarter on operations. Without meaningful revenue or financing, that spending can shorten runway quickly.

The cash balance matters, but the expense level matters too.

## How To Review Operating Expenses

A good operating expense review looks at trends and sustainability.

Useful questions include:

1. Which expense categories are largest?
2. Are expenses rising faster than revenue?
3. Did management explain the increase?
4. Are expenses one-time or recurring?
5. How do expenses affect cash runway?
6. Has the company announced cost cuts?

The goal is to understand whether expenses change financial risk.

## FAQ

### What are operating expenses?

Operating expenses are the costs a company incurs to run its business.

### Why do traders review operating expenses?

They review them to understand cash burn, net loss, cash runway, and possible financing risk.

### Are operating expenses the same as net loss?

No. Operating expenses are costs. Net loss is the bottom-line result after revenue, expenses, and other items.

### Can operating expenses be good?

They can support growth or development, but traders should review whether spending is sustainable.

### Where can traders find operating expenses?

Operating expenses are usually shown in quarterly reports, annual reports, and income statements.
