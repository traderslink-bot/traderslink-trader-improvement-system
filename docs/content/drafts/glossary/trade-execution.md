# Trade Execution

Trade execution means how a trader actually carries out a trade. It includes the entry, exit, order type, timing, fills, position changes, adds, reductions, and whether the trade followed the original plan.

A trade idea can be good while the execution is poor. A trade can also lose money even when execution was reasonable. That is why execution review matters.

## Simple Definition

Trade execution is the process of turning a trade idea into actual orders and decisions.

It can include:

1. Where the trader entered.
2. How the order was placed.
3. Whether the fill was clean.
4. How risk was managed.
5. Whether adds or reductions followed the plan.
6. Where the trader exited.
7. Whether the trade followed the original thesis.

Execution is the part of trading the trader controls most directly.

## Why Trade Execution Matters

Execution matters because small decisions can change the result of a trade.

A trader may have the right idea but still lose because:

1. The entry was late.
2. The stop was ignored.
3. Position size was too large.
4. The trader added after the setup failed.
5. The exit was emotional.
6. Profit was not protected.
7. The trade no longer matched the plan.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They blame the setup without reviewing how they handled the trade.

## Example

A trader plans to buy a breakout near resistance. Instead of entering near the level, they chase after several green candles. The stock pulls back and the trader exits red.

The trade idea may have been a breakout. The execution issue may have been the late entry and poor risk location.

That difference matters during review.

## How To Review Trade Execution

A useful review asks:

1. Was the entry planned or emotional?
2. Was risk defined before entry?
3. Did the trader follow the stop plan?
4. Did adds improve or hurt the trade?
5. Did the exit match the original plan?
6. Did the trader repeat a known mistake?

The goal is not to judge only by P&L. The goal is to understand decision quality.

## FAQ

### What does trade execution mean?

Trade execution means how a trader enters, manages, and exits a trade.

### Is trade execution the same as a trade idea?

No. A trade idea is the plan or thesis. Execution is how the trader carries out that plan.

### Why is execution quality important?

Execution quality helps traders understand whether results came from the setup, the market, or their own trade management decisions.

### Can a good idea have bad execution?

Yes. A trader can have a good idea but enter late, size poorly, ignore risk, or exit emotionally.

### How can traders improve execution?

They can review entries, exits, risk, adds, reductions, and rule-following after each trade.
