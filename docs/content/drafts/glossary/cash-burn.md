# Cash Burn

Cash burn is the rate at which a company uses cash to fund operations. Traders review cash burn because it can help estimate cash runway, financing risk, and potential dilution risk.

Cash burn can change over time. It may increase when a company spends more on research, sales, operations, or debt obligations. It may decrease if the company cuts costs or brings in more cash from operations.

## Simple Definition

Cash burn is how quickly a company spends cash.

Cash burn may be reviewed through:

1. Operating cash flow.
2. Operating expenses.
3. Net loss.
4. Cash used in operations.
5. Quarterly financial statements.
6. Annual reports.
7. Management discussion.
8. Financing updates.

For small-cap traders, cash burn can help estimate whether a company may need to raise capital.

## Why Cash Burn Matters

Cash burn matters because high spending can shorten cash runway.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may focus only on a stock catalyst and ignore that the company is burning cash quickly.

A useful review asks:

1. How much cash is the company using?
2. Is cash burn increasing or decreasing?
3. How much cash is left?
4. Does revenue offset expenses?
5. Does the company have debt or major obligations?
6. Could financing be needed soon?

Cash burn should be reviewed with cash runway and dilution risk.

## Example

A company has $10 million in cash and uses about $2 million per quarter. If spending stays similar, the company may have several quarters of runway before needing more cash.

If expenses increase, that runway can shorten.

## How To Review Cash Burn

A good cash burn review looks at trend and sustainability.

Useful questions include:

1. What was cash used in operations?
2. What are operating expenses?
3. Did spending rise from the prior period?
4. Is revenue improving?
5. Is the company cutting costs?
6. Does the burn rate create dilution risk?

The goal is to understand whether spending changes the trade thesis.

## FAQ

### What is cash burn?

Cash burn is the rate at which a company uses cash to fund operations.

### Why does cash burn matter?

Cash burn helps traders estimate how long a company can operate before needing more financing.

### Is high cash burn always bad?

Not always. Some companies spend heavily to grow, but high cash burn can increase financing risk if cash is limited.

### How is cash burn related to cash runway?

Cash runway estimates how long cash may last based on cash burn and other financial factors.

### Where can traders find cash burn information?

Cash flow statements, quarterly reports, annual reports, and management discussion sections can provide clues.
