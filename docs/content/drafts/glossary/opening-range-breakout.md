# Opening Range Breakout

An opening range breakout happens when price breaks above the high of the opening range. Traders often watch opening range breakouts because they can show early-session momentum, attention, or continuation after the first range is established.

An opening range breakout does not guarantee continuation. It can hold, fail, fake out, reject, or reverse depending on volume, context, and execution.

## Simple Definition

An opening range breakout is a move above the opening range high.

A trader first defines the opening range, such as:

1. First 1 minute.
2. First 5 minutes.
3. First 15 minutes.
4. First 30 minutes.
5. First hour.

If price breaks above the high of that range, traders may call it an opening range breakout.

## Why Opening Range Breakouts Matter

Opening range breakouts matter because they can create a clear early-session level for trade planning.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may enter before the range is fully formed, chase after the move is extended, or ignore a quick failed breakout.

A useful review asks:

1. What time window defined the opening range?
2. What was the opening range high?
3. Did price break and hold above it?
4. Did volume support the breakout?
5. Was the entry planned or late?
6. Did the trader exit if the breakout failed?

The opening range breakout should be reviewed as a structured setup, not just a fast candle.

## Example

A stock trades between $2.00 and $2.20 during the first 5 minutes. Later, price breaks above $2.20 with volume.

That move can be reviewed as a 5-minute opening range breakout. If price falls back below $2.20, the breakout may have failed.

## How To Review Opening Range Breakouts

A good review looks at the range, breakout quality, and trader response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the opening range clearly defined?
2. Did the breakout happen with volume?
3. Did price hold above the range?
4. Was the entry near the breakout or extended?
5. Was risk defined before entry?
6. Did the trader respond properly if the breakout failed?

The goal is to understand whether the ORB trade had structure or was a chase.

## FAQ

### What is an opening range breakout?

An opening range breakout happens when price breaks above the high of the opening range.

### What does ORB mean?

ORB usually means opening range breakout.

### Does an opening range breakout guarantee continuation?

No. An ORB can fail, reject, fake out, or reverse.

### What confirms an opening range breakout?

Confirmation may include volume, price holding above the opening range high, or a successful retest.

### Why do traders review ORB trades?

They review ORB trades to understand entry timing, range quality, confirmation, risk, and failed breakout behavior.
