# Time And Sales

Time and sales is a real-time record of trades that have executed. It usually shows the time, price, and size of recent trades. Traders use time and sales to review tape behavior, trade speed, liquidity, and order flow context.

Time and sales does not guarantee price direction. It shows what has traded, not what must happen next.

## Simple Definition

Time and sales shows completed trade prints.

It may show:

1. Time of each trade.
2. Price of each trade.
3. Size of each trade.
4. Trade speed.
5. Prints near the bid or ask.
6. Large trades.
7. Bursts of volume.
8. Real-time transaction flow.

It is often one of the main tools used for tape reading.

## Why Time And Sales Matters

Time and sales matters because it shows actual executed trades, not just displayed orders.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may see large prints and assume a move will continue, even if price is failing at a key level.

A useful review asks:

1. What were prints doing before entry?
2. Were trades speeding up?
3. Were large prints appearing?
4. Did price respond to the prints?
5. Did the trader chase activity?
6. Did time and sales improve or hurt execution?

Time and sales should be reviewed with chart structure and risk.

## Example

A stock approaches resistance and time and sales shows fast prints at or near the ask. That may show buying activity, but the trader still needs to review whether price actually breaks and holds above resistance.

Fast prints without price progress can sometimes signal absorption or rejection.

## How To Review Time And Sales

A good review looks at trade prints and price response.

Useful questions include:

1. Did prints support the setup?
2. Did price move with the prints?
3. Did prints slow before the trade failed?
4. Did the trader react too quickly?
5. Was the stock liquid enough?
6. Did the tape match the chart plan?

The goal is to understand whether real-time prints helped decision-making.

## FAQ

### What is time and sales?

Time and sales is a real-time record of completed trades, usually showing time, price, and size.

### Is time and sales the same as Level 2?

No. Time and sales shows completed trades. Level 2 shows displayed bid and ask depth.

### Why do traders watch time and sales?

They watch it to understand trade speed, size, prints, and real-time order flow context.

### Does time and sales predict price?

No. It shows executed trades, but it does not guarantee future price movement.

### Can time and sales be misleading?

Yes. Prints need to be reviewed with price action, level behavior, liquidity, and risk.
