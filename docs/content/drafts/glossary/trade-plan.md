# Trade Plan

A trade plan is a written or clearly defined plan for how a trader intends to enter, manage, and exit a trade. It helps turn a trade idea into a structured decision instead of an emotional reaction.

A trade plan does not guarantee that a trade will work. It gives the trader a way to define risk, review execution, and learn whether they followed their own process.

## Simple Definition

A trade plan is a set of rules and expectations for a specific trade.

A trade plan may include:

1. Trade thesis.
2. Setup name.
3. Entry area.
4. Stop or invalidation level.
5. Target area.
6. Position size.
7. Risk amount.
8. Add or scale-out rules.
9. Exit conditions.
10. Review notes.

The more specific the plan, the easier it is to review later.

## Why Trade Plans Matter

Trade plans matter because they help traders make decisions before emotions take over.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may enter a trade without knowing where they are wrong, how much they are risking, or when they should exit.

A useful review asks:

1. Was there a plan before entry?
2. Did the trader follow the plan?
3. Was the risk clear?
4. Was the invalidation level clear?
5. Did the trader change the plan emotionally?
6. What should be improved in the next plan?

A trade plan gives post-trade review something concrete to compare against.

## Example

A trader plans to buy a pullback near support, risk below the support level, take partial profit near resistance, and exit if volume fades.

After the trade, the trader can review whether the entry, stop, partial exit, and final exit followed the plan.

## How To Review A Trade Plan

A good trade plan review looks at planning and execution.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the plan specific enough?
2. Was the setup valid?
3. Did the entry match the plan?
4. Did the exit match the plan?
5. Did the trader respect invalidation?
6. Did the trader learn something useful?

The goal is not to create a perfect plan. The goal is to create a plan that can be followed and reviewed.

## FAQ

### What is a trade plan?

A trade plan is a defined plan for entering, managing, and exiting a specific trade.

### Is a trade plan the same as a trading plan?

They are related. A trading plan is usually the broader system or rules. A trade plan is often the plan for one specific trade.

### What should a trade plan include?

It can include thesis, setup, entry, stop, invalidation, target, size, risk, add rules, exit rules, and review notes.

### Why do traders need trade plans?

Trade plans help traders define risk and make decisions before emotions take over.

### Can a trade plan change during the trade?

It can if new information appears, but traders should avoid changing the plan emotionally just to avoid taking a loss.
