# Average Win

Average win is the average amount a trader makes on winning trades. It is a performance metric that helps traders understand the size of their winners compared with their losses.

Average win does not tell the whole story by itself. A trader also needs to review win rate, average loss, risk-reward, position sizing, and trade management.

## Simple Definition

Average win is calculated by adding all winning trade profits and dividing by the number of winning trades.

For example:

1. Winning trade 1: $100.
2. Winning trade 2: $150.
3. Winning trade 3: $50.
4. Total wins: $300.
5. Number of winners: 3.
6. Average win: $100.

This gives a simple view of how large winners are on average.

## Why Average Win Matters

Average win matters because it helps traders review whether their winning trades are large enough to support their strategy.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may have a high win rate but very small winners, while a few larger losses erase the gains.

A useful review asks:

1. What is the average win?
2. What is the average loss?
3. Are winners being cut too early?
4. Are targets realistic?
5. Is the trader scaling out too quickly?
6. Does average win support the strategy?

Average win is most useful when compared with average loss.

## Example

A trader wins 60% of trades, but the average win is $40 and the average loss is $120. Even with a decent win rate, the trader may struggle because losses are much larger than winners.

That does not automatically mean the strategy is bad. It means trade management and risk-reward need review.

## How To Review Average Win

A good review looks beyond the number.

Useful questions include:

1. Which setups produce the largest winners?
2. Which setups produce small winners?
3. Are winners being cut before the plan?
4. Are partial exits helping or hurting?
5. Is the trader exiting from fear?
6. Does the average win change by time of day?

The goal is to understand why winners are the size they are.

## FAQ

### What is average win in trading?

Average win is the average profit from winning trades.

### How do you calculate average win?

Add the profits from all winning trades and divide by the number of winning trades.

### Why does average win matter?

It helps traders understand whether winners are large enough compared with losses and risk.

### Is average win more important than win rate?

Neither is enough alone. Average win should be reviewed with win rate, average loss, and risk-reward.

### What can make average win too small?

Cutting winners too early, poor targets, fear-based exits, or scaling out too quickly can reduce average win.
