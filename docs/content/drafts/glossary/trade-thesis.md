# Trade Thesis

A trade thesis is the reason behind a trade idea. It explains why a trader believes a setup is worth taking, what needs to happen for the idea to remain valid, and what would prove the idea wrong.

A trade thesis is not a guarantee that the trade will work. It is a planning and review tool that helps traders avoid random entries.

## Simple Definition

A trade thesis is the core reason for taking a trade.

A thesis may include:

1. The setup.
2. The catalyst.
3. The key level.
4. The expected move.
5. The risk location.
6. The invalidation point.
7. The reason the trade should work.
8. The reason to exit if it stops working.

The more clearly the thesis is written, the easier the trade is to review later.

## Why Trade Thesis Matters

A trade thesis matters because it gives the trade a purpose.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may enter because a stock is moving, but later struggle to explain why they took the trade.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the thesis?
2. Was the thesis specific?
3. Did the trader follow the thesis?
4. Did the thesis fail?
5. Did the trader exit when the thesis failed?
6. Did the trader change the thesis after entry?

A clear thesis helps separate planned trades from impulse trades.

## Example

A trader’s thesis might be: a stock has strong news, unusual volume, and is holding above a key breakout level. The trade remains valid only while price holds that level.

If the stock loses the level and volume fades, the thesis may no longer be valid.

## How To Review A Trade Thesis

A good thesis review looks at whether the idea stayed valid and whether the trader respected it.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the thesis written before entry?
2. What would prove the thesis wrong?
3. Did the trader follow the plan?
4. Did new information change the thesis?
5. Did the trader hold after invalidation?
6. Was the trade based on structure or emotion?

The goal is to review both the idea and the behavior around the idea.

## FAQ

### What is a trade thesis?

A trade thesis is the reason or logic behind a trade idea.

### Is a trade thesis the same as a setup?

Not exactly. A setup is the pattern or situation. The thesis explains why that setup is worth trading and what would invalidate it.

### Why should traders write a thesis?

Writing a thesis helps traders define the reason for the trade and review whether the trade followed the plan.

### Can a trade thesis change?

It can change if new information appears, but traders should be careful not to change the thesis emotionally after entry.

### What makes a weak trade thesis?

A vague reason like “it is moving” or “everyone is watching it” may be too weak to review properly.
