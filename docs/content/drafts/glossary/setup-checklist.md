# Setup Checklist

A setup checklist is a list of conditions used to confirm whether a trade setup meets the trader’s requirements. It helps traders avoid taking trades that look interesting but do not actually match the plan.

A setup checklist does not guarantee that a trade will work. It helps make setup selection clearer and easier to review.

## Simple Definition

A setup checklist is a checklist for validating a specific setup.

A setup checklist may include:

1. Setup type.
2. Catalyst.
3. Volume.
4. Relative volume.
5. Key level.
6. Entry area.
7. Stop or invalidation.
8. Liquidity.
9. Risk-reward.
10. Reason to avoid the trade.

The checklist should match the setup being traded.

## Why Setup Checklists Matter

Setup checklists matter because many trades fail before entry because the setup was never clear.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may label a trade as a breakout or dip buy even though it does not meet the rules for that setup.

A useful review asks:

1. Did the setup meet the checklist?
2. Which condition was missing?
3. Did the trader force the setup?
4. Was risk clearly defined?
5. Did volume support the setup?
6. Did the checklist reduce weak trades?

A setup checklist helps traders separate real setups from excitement.

## Example

A trader has a breakout checklist requiring a clean resistance level, strong volume, a catalyst, and a defined stop. If the stock is moving but has no clean level, the checklist may keep the trader out.

That helps protect the trader from forcing a trade.

## How To Review A Setup Checklist

A good setup checklist review looks at checklist quality and trade outcomes.

Useful questions include:

1. Were the criteria specific enough?
2. Did the trader follow the checklist?
3. Did losing trades skip checklist items?
4. Did winning trades share common criteria?
5. Is the checklist too strict or too loose?
6. Should criteria be changed?

The goal is to improve setup selection over time.

## FAQ

### What is a setup checklist?

A setup checklist is a list of required conditions used to confirm whether a trade setup is valid.

### Why use a setup checklist?

A setup checklist helps traders avoid forcing trades that do not match the plan.

### Is a setup checklist the same as a trading checklist?

Not exactly. A trading checklist may cover the whole trade process. A setup checklist focuses on whether the setup itself is valid.

### What should be on a setup checklist?

It may include catalyst, volume, level quality, entry area, risk, liquidity, and risk-reward.

### Can setup checklists improve trade review?

They can make it easier to see whether trades followed the intended criteria.
