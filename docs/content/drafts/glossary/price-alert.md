# Price Alert

A price alert is a notification that fires when a stock reaches a selected price level. Traders use price alerts to monitor support, resistance, breakouts, breakdowns, premarket levels, high of day, low of day, and other trade conditions.

A price alert is not a trade signal by itself. It tells the trader that price reached a level worth reviewing.

## Simple Definition

A price alert notifies a trader when price reaches a selected level.

Price alerts may be placed near:

1. Support.
2. Resistance.
3. Premarket high.
4. Premarket low.
5. High of day.
6. Low of day.
7. Breakout levels.
8. Breakdown levels.
9. Stop or invalidation levels.
10. Target areas.

The alert should connect to a clear review plan.

## Why Price Alerts Matter

Price alerts matter because they help traders monitor important levels without staring at every chart.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may get a price alert and immediately enter without checking volume, liquidity, spread, catalyst, or confirmation.

A useful review asks:

1. What level triggered the alert?
2. Why was that level important?
3. Did price confirm or reject?
4. Was volume meaningful?
5. Did the trader chase after the alert?
6. Did the alert improve decision quality?

A price alert should start the review process, not replace it.

## Example

A trader marks $2.50 as resistance and sets a price alert at $2.48. When the alert fires, the trader checks whether volume is increasing and whether price can break and hold above resistance.

The alert helps the trader focus at the right time.

## How To Review Price Alerts

A good price alert review looks at level quality and trader response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the alert level meaningful?
2. Was it placed too close or too far away?
3. Did it trigger before the setup was ready?
4. Did the trader wait for confirmation?
5. Did the trader enter with a plan?
6. Should the alert criteria be improved?

The goal is to make price alerts useful instead of noisy.

## FAQ

### What is a price alert?

A price alert is a notification that fires when a stock reaches a selected price level.

### Is a price alert a buy or sell signal?

No. A price alert is a prompt to review price action and context.

### What levels can traders set price alerts on?

Traders may set alerts on support, resistance, high of day, low of day, breakout levels, stop levels, or target areas.

### Can price alerts reduce screen watching?

Yes. Price alerts can help traders monitor key levels without watching every chart constantly.

### Can price alerts cause chasing?

They can if a trader reacts emotionally instead of reviewing the setup and risk.
