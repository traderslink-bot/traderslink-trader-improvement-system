# Support

Support is an area where price has found buying interest, paused a decline, or bounced in the past. Traders watch support because it can help identify potential risk areas, bounce zones, or places where a trade thesis may fail if price breaks down.

Support does not guarantee that price will bounce. A support area can hold, break, fake out, or become resistance later.

## Simple Definition

Support is a price area where buyers have previously shown interest.

Support may form near:

1. Prior lows.
2. Consolidation ranges.
3. High-volume areas.
4. Moving averages.
5. Previous breakout levels.
6. Psychological prices.
7. VWAP or intraday levels.
8. Prior demand zones.

Support is usually better viewed as an area, not one perfect price.

## Why Support Matters

Support matters because it can help traders plan risk and review price behavior.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may buy near support but keep holding after support clearly breaks.

A useful review asks:

1. What support area mattered?
2. Was support tested before?
3. Did price hold or break?
4. Did volume support the bounce?
5. Did the trader define invalidation?
6. Did the trader exit if support failed?

Support is most useful when connected to a clear trade plan.

## Example

A stock falls to $2.00 several times and bounces. Traders may begin to view $2.00 as support.

If price later breaks below $2.00 and cannot reclaim it, that support may have failed.

## How To Review Support

A good support review looks at level quality and trade response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the support area clear?
2. How many times was it tested?
3. Did the trader enter near support or chase away from it?
4. Was risk defined below support?
5. Did support break?
6. Did the trader adjust properly after the break?

The goal is to understand whether support helped the trade plan or became an excuse to hold.

## FAQ

### What is support in trading?

Support is an area where price has previously found buying interest or stopped falling.

### Does support always hold?

No. Support can break, fake out, or become resistance later.

### Is support one exact price?

Not always. Many traders treat support as a zone or area rather than one exact number.

### Why do traders review support?

They review support to understand risk, invalidation, bounce quality, and trade management.

### What happens when support breaks?

A support break can signal weakness or invalidation, but it does not guarantee continued downside.

### How does Trader Intelligence relate to support?

Trader Intelligence is being built to help traders review support trades, breakdowns, execution, and trade management patterns.
