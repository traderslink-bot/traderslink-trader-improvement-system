# Bid

Bid is the highest price buyers are currently willing to pay for a stock. Traders review the bid because it affects exits, fills, liquidity, spread, and execution quality.

The bid does not guarantee a trader will be able to sell unlimited shares at that price. Bid size, liquidity, volatility, and order flow all matter.

## Simple Definition

The bid is the price buyers are offering.

For example:

1. Bid price: $2.00.
2. Ask price: $2.05.
3. Spread: $0.05.

If a trader sells with a market order, the order may fill at or near the bid depending on liquidity and speed.

## Why Bid Matters

Bid matters because it can show where buyers are currently willing to transact.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may think they can exit at a certain price, but the bid may be thin or disappear during volatility.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the bid at entry or exit?
2. Was there enough bid size?
3. Was the spread wide?
4. Did the bid disappear during the trade?
5. Did the trader exit into a weak bid?
6. Did slippage affect the result?

Bid behavior is part of execution review.

## Example

A stock shows a bid of $2.00 and an ask of $2.05. A trader holding shares may be able to sell near $2.00 if there is enough liquidity.

If the bid is thin, a larger sell order may fill lower than expected.

## How To Review Bid Behavior

A good bid review looks at liquidity and execution quality.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the bid stable?
2. Was there enough size on the bid?
3. Did the bid support price?
4. Did the bid weaken before the exit?
5. Did the trader use the right order type?
6. Did spread or slippage affect the trade?

The goal is to understand whether bid behavior helped or hurt execution.

## FAQ

### What is bid in trading?

Bid is the highest price buyers are currently willing to pay for a stock.

### Is the bid where I sell?

Often, a market sell order may fill at or near the bid, depending on liquidity and order size.

### What is bid size?

Bid size is the number of shares buyers are showing at the bid price.

### Why does bid matter for exits?

The bid helps show where buyers are available when a trader wants to sell.

### Can the bid change quickly?

Yes. The bid can change quickly during volatility, news, or low-liquidity conditions.
