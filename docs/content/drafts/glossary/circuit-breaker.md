# Circuit Breaker

A circuit breaker is a market rule that temporarily pauses trading after extreme price movement. Circuit breakers are designed to slow disorderly trading and give market participants time to process fast moves.

A circuit breaker does not guarantee that prices will stabilize after trading resumes. Markets can continue moving quickly after a pause.

## Simple Definition

A circuit breaker is a rule-based trading pause triggered by extreme movement.

Circuit breakers can apply to:

1. Individual stocks.
2. Broad market indexes.
3. Exchange-traded products.
4. Market-wide conditions.
5. Volatility bands.
6. Limit up or limit down rules.

The specific rule depends on the market and instrument.

## Why Circuit Breakers Matter

Circuit breakers matter because they can interrupt trading during high volatility.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may enter a fast-moving market without understanding that trading can pause, preventing immediate exits.

A useful review asks:

1. What triggered the circuit breaker?
2. Was the move market-wide or stock-specific?
3. Was the trader exposed during the pause?
4. How did trading resume?
5. Did spreads widen after resumption?
6. Did the trader adjust risk properly?

Circuit breaker review should focus on risk and execution.

## Example

A market index drops sharply and triggers a market-wide circuit breaker. Trading pauses across the market for a set period before resuming.

When trading resumes, the market may bounce, continue lower, or remain volatile.

## How To Review Circuit Breaker Events

A good review looks at market context and trade exposure.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the event market-wide or stock-specific?
2. What positions were open?
3. Was there a plan for a trading pause?
4. Did the trader reduce risk before or after resumption?
5. Did liquidity change after reopening?
6. Did the event affect trade discipline?

The goal is to understand whether the trader was prepared for extreme conditions.

## FAQ

### What is a circuit breaker in trading?

A circuit breaker is a market rule that pauses trading after extreme price movement.

### Is a circuit breaker the same as a trading halt?

A circuit breaker is one type of rule-based trading pause. A halt can happen for different reasons.

### Does a circuit breaker guarantee prices will recover?

No. A circuit breaker pauses trading but does not guarantee recovery or direction.

### Why do markets use circuit breakers?

They are designed to slow disorderly trading during extreme volatility.

### Can individual stocks have circuit breaker-style pauses?

Yes. Individual stocks can trigger volatility pauses under applicable market rules.
