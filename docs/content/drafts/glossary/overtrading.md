# Overtrading

Overtrading means taking too many trades, trading outside the plan, or continuing to trade when conditions no longer support good decisions. It can happen after wins, losses, boredom, frustration, or fear of missing out.

Overtrading does not only mean a high number of trades. It means the trade count or trade quality has moved away from the trader’s plan.

## Simple Definition

Overtrading means trading more than the plan supports.

Overtrading may include:

1. Taking low-quality setups.
2. Trading out of boredom.
3. Chasing scanner alerts.
4. Re-entering without a setup.
5. Trading after max loss.
6. Taking revenge trades.
7. Ignoring daily risk limits.
8. Forcing trades in poor conditions.

The issue is usually decision quality, not only trade count.

## Why Overtrading Matters

Overtrading matters because it can turn a manageable day into a much worse one.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may start with one planned trade, then keep clicking after frustration or excitement takes over.

A useful review asks:

1. How many trades were planned?
2. How many trades were taken?
3. Which trades were outside the plan?
4. Did overtrading happen after a loss?
5. Did overtrading happen after a win?
6. Did risk increase as discipline dropped?

Overtrading should be reviewed as a behavior pattern.

## Example

A trader plans to take two high-quality setups. After missing the first move, they take six lower-quality trades trying to catch up.

The issue is not only the number of trades. The issue is that the later trades were reactive and outside the plan.

## How To Review Overtrading

A good overtrading review looks at sequence and trigger points.

Useful questions include:

1. When did the trader start forcing trades?
2. What emotion was present?
3. Were the later trades lower quality?
4. Did the trader break daily risk rules?
5. Did alerts or scanner hits create pressure?
6. What rule can prevent the pattern next time?

The goal is to find the moment where discipline changed.

## FAQ

### What is overtrading?

Overtrading means taking too many trades or taking trades outside the plan.

### Is overtrading only about trade count?

No. It is also about trade quality and whether the trades match the plan.

### Why do traders overtrade?

Traders may overtrade because of boredom, frustration, excitement, FOMO, or trying to recover losses.

### How can traders review overtrading?

They can review trade sequence, setup quality, emotional triggers, risk limits, and journal notes.

### Can overtrading happen after winning?

Yes. Traders can overtrade after winning if confidence turns into impulsive decision-making.
