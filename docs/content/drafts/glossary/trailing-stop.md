# Trailing Stop

A trailing stop is a stop that moves with price by a set amount or percentage. Traders may use trailing stops to protect profit while allowing a trade to keep working if price continues in the desired direction.

A trailing stop does not guarantee the best exit or a specific fill price. Fast moves, gaps, wide spreads, and low liquidity can still create slippage.

## Simple Definition

A trailing stop follows price at a set distance.

A trailing stop may be based on:

1. Dollar amount.
2. Percentage amount.
3. Volatility measure.
4. Chart structure.
5. Moving average.
6. Manual stop adjustment.

The key idea is that the stop adjusts as price moves in favor of the trade.

## Why Trailing Stops Matter

Trailing stops matter because they can help traders protect open profit while staying in a trade.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may trail too tight and get stopped out by normal noise, or trail too loose and give back too much profit.

A useful review asks:

1. How was the trailing stop set?
2. Was it too tight for volatility?
3. Was it too loose for the trade plan?
4. Did it protect profit?
5. Did it trigger with slippage?
6. Did the trader move it emotionally?

Trailing stops should match the trade plan and market conditions.

## Example

A trader enters at $2.00 and sets a trailing stop $0.10 below the highest price reached after entry. If price rises to $2.40, the trailing stop may move to $2.30.

If price then drops to the trailing stop level, the order may trigger depending on the order type and platform settings.

## How To Review Trailing Stops

A good trailing stop review looks at profit protection and execution quality.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the trailing distance logical?
2. Did the trailing stop match the stock’s volatility?
3. Did the stop protect enough profit?
4. Did the trader get stopped out too early?
5. Did the trader give back too much open profit?
6. Was the stop automatic or manually adjusted?

The goal is to understand whether the trailing stop helped trade management.

## FAQ

### What is a trailing stop?

A trailing stop is a stop that moves with price by a set amount or percentage as the trade moves in favor.

### Does a trailing stop guarantee profit?

No. A trailing stop does not guarantee profit or a specific fill price.

### Why do traders use trailing stops?

They may use them to protect open profit while giving a trade room to continue.

### Can a trailing stop trigger too early?

Yes. If the trailing distance is too tight, normal price noise can trigger the stop.

### Can trailing stops have slippage?

Yes. Depending on the order type, liquidity, and volatility, slippage can occur.
