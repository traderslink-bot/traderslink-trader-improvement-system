# Breakout

A breakout happens when price moves above a key resistance level, range, pattern, or prior high. Traders often watch breakouts because they can signal increased demand or momentum, but a breakout does not guarantee continuation.

Breakouts should be reviewed for level quality, volume, confirmation, entry timing, and whether price holds above the breakout level.

## Simple Definition

A breakout is a move above an important level.

A breakout may happen above:

1. Resistance.
2. High of day.
3. Previous day high.
4. Chart pattern resistance.
5. Consolidation range.
6. Moving average.
7. Psychological price level.
8. News reaction high.

The level should be meaningful enough that the move can be reviewed after the trade.

## Why Breakouts Matter

Breakouts matter because they can attract attention, volume, and momentum.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may buy the breakout too late, ignore volume fade, or hold after the breakout fails.

A useful review asks:

1. What level broke?
2. Was the level meaningful?
3. Did volume confirm the move?
4. Did price hold above the level?
5. Was the entry early, planned, late, or emotional?
6. Did the trader exit if the breakout failed?

A breakout trade is not only about the level breaking. It is also about how the trader handled the move.

## Example

A stock trades under $2.00 for most of the morning. Volume increases, price moves through $2.00, and then holds above it.

That can be a breakout. If price quickly falls back under $2.00 and fails to reclaim, it may become a failed breakout.

## How To Review A Breakout

A good breakout review looks at setup quality and execution quality.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the breakout level clear?
2. Did the trader wait for confirmation?
3. Was volume strong or fading?
4. Was the entry close to the level or extended?
5. Was risk defined before entry?
6. Did the trade remain valid after entry?

The goal is to learn which breakouts are worth repeating and which ones were chase trades.

## FAQ

### What is a breakout?

A breakout is a move above a key resistance level, range, pattern, or prior high.

### Does a breakout guarantee continuation?

No. Breakouts can fail, fade, or reverse.

### What confirms a breakout?

Confirmation may include volume, a hold above the level, a retest, or price acceptance above resistance.

### What is a failed breakout?

A failed breakout happens when price moves above a level but cannot hold and falls back below it.

### Why do traders review breakout entries?

Breakout entries can become late or emotional if the trader chases after the move is already extended.
