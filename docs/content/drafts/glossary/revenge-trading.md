# Revenge Trading

Revenge trading means taking emotional trades after a loss in an attempt to make the money back quickly. It often happens when frustration, anger, embarrassment, or pressure overrides the original trading plan.

Revenge trading can increase risk because the trader is usually focused on recovery instead of setup quality.

## Simple Definition

Revenge trading is trading to get back at the market after a loss.

Revenge trading may include:

1. Re-entering immediately after a stop out.
2. Increasing size after a loss.
3. Taking low-quality setups.
4. Ignoring daily risk limits.
5. Chasing moves out of frustration.
6. Trading without confirmation.
7. Trying to recover the day quickly.
8. Breaking written rules.

The key issue is emotional decision-making after loss.

## Why Revenge Trading Matters

Revenge trading matters because one loss can turn into a much larger problem if the trader reacts emotionally.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may start with a planned trade, take a normal loss, then take several unplanned trades trying to erase it.

A useful review asks:

1. What loss triggered the behavior?
2. Was the next trade planned?
3. Did position size increase?
4. Did the trader ignore a stop?
5. Did the trader break daily risk limits?
6. What rule could stop the pattern earlier?

Revenge trading should be reviewed as a behavior sequence.

## Example

A trader loses on a breakout trade and immediately re-enters without a new setup because they want to win the money back. The second trade is not based on structure. It is based on frustration.

That is revenge trading behavior.

## How To Review Revenge Trading

A good revenge trading review looks at the trigger and the next decisions.

Useful questions include:

1. What happened right before the revenge trade?
2. Was the trader trying to recover a loss?
3. Was the setup valid?
4. Did the trader pause before re-entering?
5. Did the trader increase size emotionally?
6. What stop rule should have ended the session?

The goal is to identify the moment emotion replaced the plan.

## FAQ

### What is revenge trading?

Revenge trading is taking emotional trades after a loss to try to recover money quickly.

### Why is revenge trading risky?

It can lead to larger losses, oversized trades, broken rules, and poor setup quality.

### Is revenge trading always after a loss?

Usually it happens after a loss or missed opportunity, but the main feature is emotional recovery-focused trading.

### How can traders review revenge trading?

They can review what triggered the behavior, whether the next trade was planned, and whether risk rules were broken.

### Can a revenge trade win?

Yes, but a winning result does not make the behavior disciplined or repeatable.
