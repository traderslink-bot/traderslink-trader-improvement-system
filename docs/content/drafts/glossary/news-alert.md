# News Alert

A news alert is a notification that fires when new information appears about a stock, company, sector, or market. Traders use news alerts to monitor catalysts, filings, press releases, earnings, FDA updates, contracts, offerings, and other events.

A news alert is not a trade signal by itself. It tells the trader that new information may need review.

## Simple Definition

A news alert notifies a trader when new information appears.

News alerts may be based on:

1. Press releases.
2. SEC filings.
3. Earnings reports.
4. Contract news.
5. FDA updates.
6. Offering announcements.
7. Merger updates.
8. Analyst changes.
9. Sector news.
10. Watchlist tickers.

The alert should start a review process, not replace one.

## Why News Alerts Matter

News alerts matter because catalysts can change volume, price action, and trader attention quickly.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may react to a headline without reading the details, checking the filing, or reviewing whether price action confirms the news.

A useful review asks:

1. What news triggered the alert?
2. Was the news new and material?
3. Did the alert arrive quickly enough?
4. Did volume increase?
5. Did price hold the reaction?
6. Did the trader chase the headline?

News alerts should support catalyst review and risk control.

## Example

A trader receives a news alert that a company announced a new contract. Before trading, the trader checks whether the customer is named, whether financial terms are included, and whether volume confirms attention.

The alert helps identify the catalyst, but the trade still needs a plan.

## How To Review News Alerts

A good news alert review looks at speed, quality, and trader response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the alert timely?
2. Was the source reliable?
3. Did the alert include enough detail?
4. Did filings add important context?
5. Did the trader wait for setup confirmation?
6. Did the alert improve decision quality?

The goal is to understand whether news alerts helped the process or created emotional reactions.

## FAQ

### What is a news alert?

A news alert is a notification that fires when new information appears about a stock, company, sector, or market.

### Is a news alert a trade signal?

No. A news alert is a prompt to review the information, not an automatic trade signal.

### What can trigger a news alert?

Press releases, filings, earnings, FDA updates, contracts, offerings, analyst changes, and sector news can trigger alerts.

### Why should traders read beyond the headline?

Headlines can miss important details such as terms, dilution risk, timing, or filing context.

### Can news alerts cause chasing?

Yes. Traders can chase if they react to the alert without reviewing price action and risk.
