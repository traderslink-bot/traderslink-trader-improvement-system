# Catalyst Trader

A catalyst trader focuses on stocks moving because of news, filings, earnings, contracts, FDA updates, offerings, or other events. Catalyst traders review the quality of the catalyst, the market reaction, volume, liquidity, and risk.

Catalyst trading does not guarantee continuation. A stock can spike on news and still fade if the details are weak, the move is extended, or traders sell the reaction.

## Simple Definition

A catalyst trader trades around events or news.

Catalyst traders may focus on:

1. Press releases.
2. SEC filings.
3. Earnings updates.
4. Contract news.
5. FDA catalysts.
6. Offerings or dilution risk.
7. News alerts.
8. Volume spikes.
9. Price reaction.
10. Follow-through after the event.

The goal is to understand whether the event actually changes the trade context.

## Why Catalyst Trader Review Matters

Catalyst trader review matters because headlines can be misleading or incomplete.

This is where catalyst traders can get into trouble. They may react to a headline without reading the details, checking filings, or waiting to see whether price and volume confirm the reaction.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the catalyst?
2. Was it new and meaningful?
3. Did filings add important context?
4. Did volume confirm attention?
5. Did price hold the reaction?
6. Did the trader chase the headline?

Catalyst review should compare the quality of the news with the quality of the trade.

## Example

A company announces contract news and the stock spikes. A catalyst trader may review whether the customer is named, whether financial terms are disclosed, and whether volume supports the move.

If the headline is vague and price fades quickly, the catalyst may not have supported the trade thesis.

## How Catalyst Traders Can Review Performance

A good catalyst trading review looks at news quality and execution.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the catalyst specific or vague?
2. Did the trader read beyond the headline?
3. Did filings reveal dilution risk?
4. Was the entry planned or reactive?
5. Did the stock hold key levels?
6. Which catalyst types worked best?

The goal is to separate meaningful catalysts from noisy headlines.

## FAQ

### What is a catalyst trader?

A catalyst trader focuses on stocks moving because of news, filings, earnings, contracts, regulatory events, or other catalysts.

### Is catalyst trading the same as news trading?

They are closely related. News trading is one type of catalyst trading.

### What should catalyst traders review?

They should review catalyst quality, filings, volume, liquidity, price reaction, dilution risk, and execution timing.

### Does a catalyst guarantee a stock will move higher?

No. A catalyst does not guarantee direction or continuation.

### Why should catalyst traders read filings?

Filings may reveal terms, risk factors, dilution, financing details, or context missing from the headline.
