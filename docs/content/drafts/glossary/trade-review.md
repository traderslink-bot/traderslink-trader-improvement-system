# Trade Review

Trade review is the process of analyzing completed trades to understand what happened, what decisions were made, and what patterns should be repeated or improved.

A trade review is not only about whether the trade won or lost. A winning trade can have poor execution, and a losing trade can still follow a good plan.

## Simple Definition

Trade review means studying a trade after it is completed.

A trade review may look at:

1. Trade thesis.
2. Setup quality.
3. Entry price.
4. Exit price.
5. Risk plan.
6. Position size.
7. Trade management.
8. Execution quality.
9. Emotional decisions.
10. Final result.

The goal is to learn from the process, not only the outcome.

## Why Trade Review Matters

Trade review matters because traders often repeat the same mistakes without noticing them.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may only review big losses and ignore small habits that slowly hurt performance.

A useful review asks:

1. Was the trade planned?
2. Did the entry match the setup?
3. Was risk respected?
4. Was the exit disciplined?
5. Did the trader chase or hesitate?
6. What pattern repeated?

Trade review turns trade history into feedback.

## Example

A trader makes money on a breakout trade but entered late and ignored the original risk plan. The result was green, but the review may still flag poor execution.

That kind of review helps separate outcome from process.

## How To Review Trades

A good trade review looks at facts first, then interpretation.

Useful questions include:

1. What happened on the chart?
2. What did the trader do?
3. What was planned?
4. What changed during the trade?
5. What should be repeated?
6. What should be corrected?

The goal is to build a more consistent decision process.

## FAQ

### What is trade review?

Trade review is the process of analyzing completed trades to understand decisions, execution, risk, and outcomes.

### Should traders review winning trades?

Yes. Winning trades can still reveal poor execution, bad habits, or lucky outcomes.

### What should be included in a trade review?

A review can include setup, thesis, entry, exit, risk, size, management, emotions, and result.

### How often should traders review trades?

Traders may review trades daily, weekly, monthly, or after specific trade types depending on their process.

### Is trade review the same as performance review?

Not exactly. Trade review often focuses on individual trades, while performance review looks at broader results over a group of trades.
