# Cash Runway

Cash runway estimates how long a company can keep operating with its current cash and cash burn. Traders review cash runway because it can help identify financing risk, dilution risk, and whether a company may need to raise money.

Cash runway is only an estimate. It can change if expenses rise, revenue changes, financing happens, debt is repaid, or the company cuts costs.

## Simple Definition

Cash runway is the estimated amount of time a company can operate before needing more cash.

A simple version uses:

1. Cash on hand.
2. Cash burn rate.
3. Operating expenses.
4. Revenue or cash inflows.
5. Debt obligations.
6. Planned spending.

For traders, cash runway is often reviewed after quarterly or annual filings.

## Why Cash Runway Matters

Cash runway matters because companies with short runway may need financing.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may trade a strong catalyst without checking whether the company has enough cash to fund operations.

A useful review asks:

1. How much cash does the company have?
2. How much cash is being used each quarter?
3. How long can the company operate?
4. Does management mention funding needs?
5. Is there an active shelf or ATM program?
6. Could financing create dilution risk?

Cash runway helps connect financial condition to trading risk.

## Example

A company has $6 million in cash and burns about $2 million per quarter. A simple estimate suggests about three quarters of runway, before considering changes in revenue, expenses, debt, or financing.

The estimate should be checked against filings and management commentary.

## How To Review Cash Runway

A good cash runway review looks at cash, spending, and financing options.

Useful questions include:

1. What is current cash on hand?
2. What is quarterly cash burn?
3. Are expenses increasing?
4. Is revenue meaningful?
5. Does the company have debt due soon?
6. Has the company recently filed financing documents?

The goal is to understand whether cash needs could affect the trade thesis.

## FAQ

### What is cash runway?

Cash runway is the estimated amount of time a company can operate with current cash and cash burn.

### How do traders estimate cash runway?

They often compare cash on hand with cash burn, expenses, debt, and expected funding needs.

### Why does cash runway matter for small-cap stocks?

Short runway can increase financing or dilution risk.

### Can cash runway change quickly?

Yes. Revenue, expenses, financing, debt, or cost cuts can change runway.

### Where can traders find cash runway clues?

Quarterly reports, annual reports, cash flow statements, and management discussion sections can provide clues.
