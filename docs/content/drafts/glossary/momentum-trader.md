# Momentum Trader

A momentum trader looks for stocks showing strong price movement, volume, attention, or catalyst-driven action. Momentum traders often focus on breakouts, high relative volume, news reactions, and fast-moving intraday opportunities.

Momentum trading does not guarantee continuation. Fast moves can reverse quickly, especially when traders chase after the move is already extended.

## Simple Definition

A momentum trader trades strong movement and attention.

Momentum traders may focus on:

1. Breakouts.
2. High of day moves.
3. Volume spikes.
4. News catalysts.
5. Gap-up stocks.
6. Scanner alerts.
7. Relative volume.
8. Low-float volatility.
9. Intraday trend.
10. Fast execution.

The challenge is managing risk during fast movement.

## Why Momentum Trader Review Matters

Momentum trader review matters because momentum can create both opportunity and emotional decision-making.

This is where momentum traders can get into trouble. They may buy after a large move because they fear missing out, then get caught in a fakeout or rejection.

A useful review asks:

1. Was the move supported by volume?
2. Was there a catalyst?
3. Was the entry planned or chased?
4. Was the stock already extended?
5. Did the trader define risk?
6. Did the trader exit when momentum failed?

Momentum trading review should focus on entry quality and risk control.

## Example

A stock gaps up on news and breaks high of day with strong volume. A momentum trader may watch for continuation, but still needs a plan for rejection, fakeout, or halt risk.

The setup is only useful if risk is controlled.

## How Momentum Traders Can Review Performance

A good momentum review looks at movement quality and execution.

Useful questions include:

1. Did volume confirm the move?
2. Was the entry close to structure?
3. Did the trader chase far above the level?
4. Did spread or slippage affect the trade?
5. Did the trader respect the exit plan?
6. Which momentum setups repeated best?

The goal is to separate high-quality momentum from emotional chase trades.

## FAQ

### What is a momentum trader?

A momentum trader looks for stocks with strong price movement, volume, attention, or catalyst-driven action.

### Is momentum trading the same as day trading?

Not always. Many momentum traders day trade, but momentum strategies can also be used over longer periods.

### What do momentum traders watch?

They often watch volume, relative volume, catalysts, breakouts, high-of-day levels, liquidity, and price speed.

### Why is momentum trading risky?

Momentum can reverse quickly, especially after extended moves, fakeouts, or failed breakouts.

### How should momentum trades be reviewed?

They can be reviewed for volume, catalyst, entry timing, risk, slippage, exit discipline, and whether the trader chased.
