# Dip Buyer

A dip buyer looks for pullbacks, drops, or intraday weakness that may create a bounce or entry near support. Dip buyers often focus on support levels, reclaim behavior, volume, risk, and whether the move is a normal pullback or a failed setup.

Dip buying does not guarantee a bounce. A dip can keep dipping, break support, or turn into a larger downtrend.

## Simple Definition

A dip buyer tries to buy after price pulls back.

Dip buyers may focus on:

1. Support levels.
2. Pullbacks.
3. Reclaims.
4. Volume behavior.
5. Previous breakout levels.
6. VWAP or moving averages.
7. Risk below support.
8. Failed bounce risk.
9. Entry timing.
10. Trade management.

The goal is to enter near a planned risk area, not randomly buy weakness.

## Why Dip Buyer Review Matters

Dip buyer review matters because not every drop is a buying opportunity.

This is where dip buyers can get into trouble. They may add to a losing trade, average down after support breaks, or assume a bounce must happen.

A useful review asks:

1. What support level mattered?
2. Was the pullback normal or a breakdown?
3. Did price reclaim a key level?
4. Was risk clearly defined?
5. Did the trader add after invalidation?
6. Did the bounce actually confirm?

Dip buying review should focus on structure and risk.

## Example

A stock breaks out above $2.00 and later pulls back to retest that level. A dip buyer may watch for $2.00 to hold and for price to reclaim strength.

If $2.00 breaks and the stock fails to reclaim, the dip idea may no longer be valid.

## How Dip Buyers Can Review Performance

A good dip-buying review looks at level quality and response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the dip near real support?
2. Did the trader wait for confirmation?
3. Was the entry too early?
4. Did the trader average down emotionally?
5. Did support break?
6. Which dip setups worked best?

The goal is to separate planned pullback entries from emotional bottom-picking.

## FAQ

### What is a dip buyer?

A dip buyer looks for pullbacks or drops that may create a bounce or entry near support.

### Is buying the dip always safe?

No. A dip can continue lower or break support.

### What do dip buyers watch?

They often watch support, reclaim behavior, volume, prior breakout levels, VWAP, and risk.

### What is the risk of dip buying?

The risk is buying too early, averaging down, or holding after support fails.

### How should dip-buying trades be reviewed?

They can be reviewed for support quality, confirmation, entry timing, invalidation, and trade management.

Trader Intelligence is being built to help dip buyers review execution, support behavior, risk, and repeated decision patterns.
