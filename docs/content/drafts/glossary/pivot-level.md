# Pivot Level

A pivot level is a price area where traders watch for a possible change in behavior. Price may react near a pivot level by bouncing, rejecting, breaking out, breaking down, reclaiming, or losing momentum.

A pivot level does not guarantee a turn or continuation. It is a reviewable area where traders can plan risk, entries, exits, and trade management.

## Simple Definition

A pivot level is an important price area that may influence trade decisions.

Pivot levels may come from:

1. Prior highs.
2. Prior lows.
3. Support levels.
4. Resistance levels.
5. VWAP.
6. Moving averages.
7. Opening range levels.
8. High-volume price areas.
9. Previous breakout or breakdown areas.
10. Psychological prices.

The meaning of a pivot depends on the setup and timeframe.

## Why Pivot Levels Matter

Pivot levels matter because they can help traders organize a trade plan.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may treat every marked level as important without reviewing whether price actually reacted there.

A useful review asks:

1. What pivot level mattered?
2. Why was it marked?
3. Did price react there?
4. Did price hold, reject, break, or reclaim?
5. Was the entry near the pivot or extended away from it?
6. Did the trader manage risk around the level?

A pivot level should help clarify decisions, not create confusion.

## Example

A stock breaks above a prior high, pulls back to that area, and then holds. Traders may treat that prior high as a pivot level because price behavior changed around it.

If price loses that level later, the trade thesis may need review.

## How To Review A Pivot Level

A good pivot level review looks at why the level mattered and how the trade responded.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the pivot level based on real price history?
2. Did the level act like support or resistance?
3. Did the trader wait for confirmation?
4. Was risk defined around the level?
5. Did the trader chase far from the pivot?
6. Did price behavior at the pivot match the thesis?

The goal is to understand whether the level helped the trader make better decisions.

## FAQ

### What is a pivot level?

A pivot level is an important price area where traders watch for a possible change in price behavior.

### Is a pivot level the same as support or resistance?

It can act as support or resistance, but the term pivot level is often used more broadly for a key decision area.

### Does a pivot level guarantee a reversal?

No. Pivot levels do not guarantee reversals, breakouts, or continuation.

### How do traders use pivot levels?

They may use them for entries, exits, stops, targets, confirmation, or trade management review.

### What makes a pivot level important?

A pivot level becomes more meaningful when price has reacted there before or when it connects to a clear setup.
