# Day Trader

A day trader opens and closes trades within the same trading day. Day traders usually focus on intraday price movement, volume, catalysts, levels, liquidity, and fast execution decisions.

Day trading does not guarantee quick profits. It can involve high risk because decisions happen quickly and mistakes can compound within one session.

## Simple Definition

A day trader trades within the same day.

Day traders may focus on:

1. Intraday charts.
2. News catalysts.
3. Gap-ups and gap-downs.
4. High of day and low of day.
5. Opening range levels.
6. Volume spikes.
7. Scanner alerts.
8. Risk limits.
9. Trade journaling.
10. Execution review.

The goal is to manage short-term opportunities without carrying the trade overnight.

## Why Day Trader Review Matters

Day trader review matters because intraday trading can move quickly.

This is where day traders can get into trouble. They may chase a fast move, overtrade after losses, or ignore daily risk limits.

A useful review asks:

1. What setup was traded?
2. Was the entry planned?
3. Was risk defined before entry?
4. Did the trader follow daily loss limits?
5. Did execution quality affect the result?
6. Did the trader overtrade?

Day trading review should focus on speed, discipline, and risk.

## Example

A day trader watches a stock with news and high relative volume. The trader marks premarket high, waits for confirmation, enters near a planned level, and exits before the close.

The review should compare the actual behavior with the intraday plan.

## How Day Traders Can Review Performance

A good day trader review looks at setups and behavior by session.

Useful questions include:

1. Which time of day performed best?
2. Which setups worked?
3. Did losses happen from chasing?
4. Did the trader stop after max loss?
5. Were alerts helpful or distracting?
6. Did execution improve over time?

The goal is to find repeatable intraday strengths and weaknesses.

## FAQ

### What is a day trader?

A day trader opens and closes trades within the same trading day.

### Is day trading the same as active trading?

Day trading is one type of active trading. Active trading can also include swing trading and other frequent strategies.

### What should day traders review?

Day traders often review intraday setups, entries, exits, risk, execution, trade frequency, and discipline.

### Why is risk management important for day traders?

Intraday losses can compound quickly if position size, stops, and daily limits are not controlled.

### Do day traders hold overnight?

By definition, day traders typically close positions before the trading day ends.

### How does Trader Intelligence relate to day traders?

Trader Intelligence is being built to help day traders review execution, intraday decisions, risk, and repeated patterns.
