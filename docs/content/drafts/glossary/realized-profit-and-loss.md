# Realized Profit And Loss

Realized profit and loss, often called realized P&L, is the profit or loss from trades that have been closed. Once a position is closed, the gain or loss is no longer only theoretical. It becomes realized.

Realized P&L is important, but it does not explain the full quality of the trade. A trader still needs to review execution, risk, trade management, and whether the trade followed the plan.

## Simple Definition

Realized profit and loss is the result from closed trades.

For example:

1. A trader buys a stock at $2.00.
2. The trader sells at $2.20.
3. The profit becomes realized after the sale.

If the trader sells below the entry price, the loss becomes realized after the exit.

## Why Realized P&L Matters

Realized P&L matters because it shows the actual outcome of completed trades.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may focus only on realized P&L and ignore whether the process was good or bad.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the realized P&L?
2. Did the trade follow the plan?
3. Was the exit planned or emotional?
4. Did the trader cut the trade too early?
5. Did the trader hold too long?
6. Did fees or slippage affect the result?

Realized P&L shows what happened financially. Review explains how it happened.

## Example

A trader exits a trade for a $300 profit. That $300 is realized profit. Another trader exits a trade for a $150 loss. That $150 is realized loss.

The number matters, but the review should also look at whether the trade was managed properly.

## Realized vs Unrealized P&L

Realized P&L comes from closed trades. Unrealized P&L comes from open positions that have not been closed yet.

A trader may have unrealized profit while holding a position, but that profit can change before the trade is closed.

Once the position is closed, the result becomes realized.

## How To Review Realized P&L

A good review looks at the result and the decision path.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the realized result planned?
2. Did the exit match the trade thesis?
3. Did the trader protect profit?
4. Did the trader allow a planned loss or an oversized loss?
5. Was the result affected by poor execution?
6. What should be repeated or avoided next time?

The goal is to connect the final result with the trade decisions that created it.

## FAQ

### What is realized profit and loss?

Realized profit and loss is the profit or loss from trades that have been closed.

### Is realized P&L the same as unrealized P&L?

No. Realized P&L comes from closed trades. Unrealized P&L comes from open positions.

### Why does realized P&L matter?

It shows the actual completed result of trades.

### Can realized P&L hide poor execution?

Yes. A profitable trade can still have poor execution, and a losing trade can still follow a good plan.

### Should traders review realized P&L by setup?

Yes. Reviewing realized P&L by setup can help identify which strategies are helping or hurting results.
