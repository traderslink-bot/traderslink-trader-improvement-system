# Low Of Day

Low of day is the lowest price a stock reaches during the current trading day. Traders often watch low of day because it can become a support level, breakdown level, reclaim level, or risk reference.

Low of day does not guarantee a bounce or continued downside. A stock can break low of day and keep fading, reclaim the level, fake out, or trade sideways.

## Simple Definition

Low of day is the lowest traded price during the current trading day.

Traders may use low of day as:

1. A support level.
2. A breakdown level.
3. A weakness reference.
4. A reclaim level.
5. A target area.
6. A scanner condition.
7. A trade management marker.
8. A level for review after the trade.

The meaning depends on volume, context, and how price behaves near the level.

## Why Low Of Day Matters

Low of day matters because many traders watch it as a sign of intraday weakness.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may hold a long trade after low of day breaks or short a breakdown after the move is already extended.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the low-of-day level?
2. Did price break below it?
3. Did volume support the break?
4. Did price reclaim the level?
5. Was the entry planned or late?
6. Did the trader manage risk if the level failed?

Low of day should be reviewed with structure, not treated as a signal by itself.

## Example

A stock trades down to $1.80 during the morning and bounces. Later, it returns to $1.80 and breaks below it.

That move may be a low-of-day breakdown. If price quickly reclaims $1.80, it may become a fakeout or reclaim.

## How To Review Low Of Day

A good low-of-day review looks at level quality, volume, and execution.

Useful questions include:

1. Was low of day clear?
2. Was the stock already extended lower?
3. Did the trader wait for confirmation?
4. Did the entry happen near the level or far below it?
5. Did the breakdown hold?
6. Did the trader manage risk after reclaim?

The goal is to understand whether the low-of-day trade had structure or was a reaction.

## FAQ

### What is low of day?

Low of day is the lowest price a stock reaches during the current trading day.

### Why do traders watch low of day?

They watch it because it can act as a support level, breakdown level, reclaim level, target, or risk reference.

### Does a low-of-day break guarantee more downside?

No. Price can break low of day and still reclaim, fake out, or trade sideways.

### What is LOD trading?

LOD usually means low of day. LOD trading often refers to watching or trading around the low-of-day level.
