# Active Trader

An active trader is someone who trades frequently and actively monitors markets, setups, positions, and risk. Active traders may day trade, swing trade, scalp, trade momentum, or use multiple short-term strategies.

Being an active trader does not guarantee better results. More trades can create more opportunities, but also more chances for mistakes, fees, slippage, and emotional decisions.

## Simple Definition

An active trader is a trader who participates frequently in the market.

Active traders may focus on:

1. Intraday setups.
2. Swing trades.
3. Momentum moves.
4. News catalysts.
5. Technical levels.
6. Short-term risk management.
7. Trade journaling.
8. Performance review.
9. Execution quality.
10. Repeatable trading rules.

The key feature is frequent and intentional market participation.

## Why Active Trader Review Matters

Active trader review matters because frequent trading creates many decisions to evaluate.

This is where active traders can get into trouble. They may trade often but fail to review whether the trades are high quality or just reactive.

A useful review asks:

1. How many trades were planned?
2. How many trades were taken?
3. Which setups performed best?
4. Did fees and slippage matter?
5. Did overtrading appear?
6. Did risk stay controlled?

Active trading needs strong review habits.

## Example

An active trader takes 40 trades in a month. After reviewing the trades, they find that only two setups are profitable and the rest are noise.

That review can help the trader focus on what is working.

## How Active Traders Can Review Performance

A good active trader review looks at volume of decisions and quality of decisions.

Useful questions include:

1. Were trades tagged by setup?
2. Did trade frequency improve results?
3. Did execution costs hurt performance?
4. Did risk increase during losing streaks?
5. Did alerts and scanners create too much noise?
6. What should be reduced or repeated?

The goal is to make active trading more intentional and less reactive.

## FAQ

### What is an active trader?

An active trader is someone who trades frequently and actively monitors markets, setups, positions, and risk.

### Is active trading the same as day trading?

Not always. Day trading is one form of active trading, but active traders may also swing trade or use other strategies.

### What should active traders review?

Active traders often review execution, setup performance, risk, fees, slippage, trade frequency, and behavior patterns.

### Can active trading lead to overtrading?

Yes. Frequent trading can become overtrading if trades stop matching the plan.

### Why is journaling important for active traders?

Journaling helps active traders find patterns across many trades and decisions.

### How does Trader Intelligence relate to active traders?

Trader Intelligence is being built to help active traders review execution, performance, risk, and repeated decision patterns.
