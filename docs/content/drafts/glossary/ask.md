# Ask

Ask is the lowest price sellers are currently willing to accept for a stock. Traders review the ask because it affects entries, fills, liquidity, spread, and execution quality.

The ask does not guarantee a trader can buy unlimited shares at that price. Ask size, liquidity, volatility, and order flow all matter.

## Simple Definition

The ask is the price sellers are offering.

For example:

1. Bid price: $2.00.
2. Ask price: $2.05.
3. Spread: $0.05.

If a trader buys with a market order, the order may fill at or near the ask depending on liquidity and speed.

## Why Ask Matters

Ask matters because it can show where sellers are currently willing to transact.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may think they can enter at a certain price, but the ask may be thin or move higher during volatility.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the ask at entry?
2. Was there enough ask size?
3. Was the spread wide?
4. Did the trader chase the ask?
5. Did the ask move before the fill?
6. Did slippage affect the result?

Ask behavior is part of execution review.

## Example

A stock shows a bid of $2.00 and an ask of $2.05. A trader buying shares may be able to buy near $2.05 if there is enough liquidity.

If the ask is thin, a larger buy order may fill higher than expected.

## How To Review Ask Behavior

A good ask review looks at liquidity and execution quality.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the ask stable?
2. Was there enough size on the ask?
3. Did the ask lift quickly?
4. Did the trader chase a moving ask?
5. Did the trader use the right order type?
6. Did spread or slippage affect the trade?

The goal is to understand whether ask behavior helped or hurt execution.

## FAQ

### What is ask in trading?

Ask is the lowest price sellers are currently willing to accept for a stock.

### Is the ask where I buy?

Often, a market buy order may fill at or near the ask, depending on liquidity and order size.

### What is ask size?

Ask size is the number of shares sellers are showing at the ask price.

### Why does ask matter for entries?

The ask helps show where sellers are available when a trader wants to buy.

### Can the ask change quickly?

Yes. The ask can change quickly during volatility, news, or low-liquidity conditions.
