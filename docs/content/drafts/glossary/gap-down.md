# Gap Down

A gap down happens when a stock opens lower than its previous closing price. Traders often watch gap-down stocks because they can show negative news reaction, weak sentiment, selling pressure, or broader market weakness.

A gap down does not guarantee continued downside. A stock can gap down and keep fading, bounce after the open, reclaim levels, or trade sideways.

## Simple Definition

A gap down means the stock opens below the prior closing price.

A gap down can happen because of:

1. Bad news.
2. Earnings disappointment.
3. SEC filings.
4. Offering announcements.
5. Analyst downgrades.
6. Market weakness.
7. Sector weakness.
8. Premarket selling.

The reason for the gap matters during trade review.

## Why Gap Downs Matter

Gap downs matter because they can create risk and opportunity at the same time.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may panic sell without a plan, short too late, or assume the gap down must continue.

A useful review asks:

1. Why did the stock gap down?
2. Was the catalyst serious?
3. How much did it move premarket?
4. Did it hold or lose premarket levels?
5. Did volume support the move?
6. Did the trader react emotionally?

A gap down should be reviewed with context, not treated as a signal by itself.

## Example

A stock closes at $2.00 and opens the next day at $1.60 after an offering announcement. That is a gap down.

If the stock loses premarket low and volume expands, weakness may continue. If it reclaims key levels, the gap-down move may stabilize or reverse.

## How To Review A Gap Down

A good gap-down review looks at the catalyst, levels, and execution.

Useful questions include:

1. What caused the gap?
2. Was the move already extended?
3. What was the premarket high?
4. What was the premarket low?
5. Did the stock reclaim key levels?
6. Did the trader have a clear plan?

The goal is to understand whether the trader traded structure or reacted emotionally to the gap.

## FAQ

### What is a gap down?

A gap down happens when a stock opens below its previous closing price.

### Does a gap down mean the stock will keep falling?

No. A gap down can continue lower, bounce, reclaim levels, or trade sideways.

### Why do stocks gap down?

Stocks can gap down because of bad news, earnings disappointment, offerings, filings, market weakness, or premarket selling.

### What should traders review on a gap down?

Traders often review catalyst quality, premarket high, premarket low, volume, liquidity, and entry or exit timing.

### Can a gap down create a bounce trade?

It can, but a bounce is not guaranteed. Traders still need a plan and risk control.
