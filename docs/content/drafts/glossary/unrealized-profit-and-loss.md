# Unrealized Profit And Loss

Unrealized profit and loss, often called unrealized P&L, is the profit or loss on an open position that has not been closed yet. It changes as the market price changes.

Unrealized P&L is important, but it is not locked in. A trade can show unrealized profit and later close for less profit, breakeven, or a loss if the position is not managed well.

## Simple Definition

Unrealized profit and loss is the current gain or loss on an open trade.

For example:

1. A trader buys a stock at $2.00.
2. The stock trades at $2.20.
3. The open position shows unrealized profit.
4. The profit becomes realized only if the trader sells and closes the trade.

Until the trade is closed, the number can change.

## Why Unrealized P&L Matters

Unrealized P&L matters because it can affect trader behavior. Seeing open profit can cause fear, greed, hesitation, or emotional decision-making.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may let a green trade turn red, exit too early from fear, or hold a losing open position because they do not want to realize the loss.

A useful review asks:

1. Did the trader protect unrealized profit?
2. Did the trader follow the exit plan?
3. Did open P&L affect emotions?
4. Did the trader hold after the thesis failed?
5. Did the trader move stops emotionally?
6. Did the trade management match the plan?

Unrealized P&L is a number, but the behavior around it matters.

## Example

A trader is up $500 on an open trade. The trader has not sold, so the profit is unrealized. If the stock reverses and the trader exits for $100, the realized profit is $100, not $500.

That difference is important in trade review.

## Unrealized vs Realized P&L

Unrealized P&L is tied to open positions. Realized P&L is tied to closed trades.

Unrealized P&L can change every second while the position is open. Realized P&L becomes fixed once the trade is closed, before later adjustments such as fees or corrections.

## How To Review Unrealized P&L

A good review looks at how the trader managed open profit or open loss.

Useful questions include:

1. Did the trader have a plan for open profit?
2. Did the trader protect gains when the trade moved in favor?
3. Did the trader let a planned small loss become larger?
4. Did unrealized P&L cause emotional decisions?
5. Did the exit match structure or emotion?
6. What should be improved next time?

The goal is to understand how the trade was managed while it was still open.

## FAQ

### What is unrealized profit and loss?

Unrealized profit and loss is the current profit or loss on an open position that has not been closed.

### Is unrealized P&L locked in?

No. Unrealized P&L changes as price changes and becomes realized only when the position is closed.

### What is the difference between unrealized and realized P&L?

Unrealized P&L is for open positions. Realized P&L is for closed trades.

### Why does unrealized P&L matter?

It can affect trade management, emotions, profit protection, and loss control.

### Can unrealized profit turn into a realized loss?

Yes. If a position reverses and the trader exits below the entry price, unrealized profit can turn into a realized loss.
