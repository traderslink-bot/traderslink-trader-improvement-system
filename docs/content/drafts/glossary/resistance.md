# Resistance

Resistance is an area where price has met selling pressure, paused a move higher, or failed to continue in the past. Traders watch resistance because it can help identify breakout levels, profit-taking areas, or places where a trade may reject.

Resistance does not guarantee that price will fall. A resistance area can reject, break out, fake out, or later become support.

## Simple Definition

Resistance is a price area where sellers have previously shown interest.

Resistance may form near:

1. Prior highs.
2. Consolidation ranges.
3. High-volume areas.
4. Moving averages.
5. Previous breakdown levels.
6. Psychological prices.
7. VWAP or intraday levels.
8. Prior supply zones.

Resistance is usually better viewed as an area, not one perfect price.

## Why Resistance Matters

Resistance matters because it can help traders plan entries, exits, risk, and targets.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may buy directly into resistance without a plan, then hold after price rejects.

A useful review asks:

1. What resistance area mattered?
2. Was the level tested before?
3. Did price reject or break out?
4. Did volume support the breakout?
5. Did the trader define invalidation?
6. Did the trader exit if resistance rejected?

Resistance is most useful when connected to a clear trade plan.

## Example

A stock rallies to $2.50 several times and fails. Traders may begin to view $2.50 as resistance.

If price later breaks above $2.50 and holds, that resistance may become a breakout level.

## How To Review Resistance

A good resistance review looks at level quality and trade response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the resistance area clear?
2. How many times was it tested?
3. Did the trader enter near resistance or wait for breakout confirmation?
4. Was risk defined if the level rejected?
5. Did price break out or fail?
6. Did the trader adjust properly after rejection?

The goal is to understand whether resistance helped the trade plan or became a place where the trader chased.

## FAQ

### What is resistance in trading?

Resistance is an area where price has previously met selling pressure or stopped rising.

### Does resistance always reject price?

No. Resistance can reject, break out, fake out, or later become support.

### Is resistance one exact price?

Not always. Many traders treat resistance as a zone or area rather than one exact number.

### Why do traders review resistance?

They review resistance to understand breakout quality, rejection, target areas, and trade management.

### What happens when resistance breaks?

A resistance break can become a breakout, but it does not guarantee continuation.
