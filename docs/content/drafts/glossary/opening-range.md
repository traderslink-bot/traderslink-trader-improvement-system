# Opening Range

Opening range is the high and low formed during the first part of the regular trading session. Traders often use the opening range to define early support, resistance, breakout levels, breakdown levels, and market direction context.

The opening range does not guarantee a breakout or breakdown will work. It is a reference area that should be reviewed with volume, catalyst, market context, and execution.

## Simple Definition

Opening range is the price range created during the first selected window of the trading day.

Common opening range windows include:

1. First 1 minute.
2. First 5 minutes.
3. First 15 minutes.
4. First 30 minutes.
5. First hour.

The trader should define the time window before using the range.

## Why Opening Range Matters

Opening range matters because the early session can set important intraday levels.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may trade a supposed opening range breakout without defining the range or waiting for a real break.

A useful review asks:

1. What time window defined the range?
2. What was the opening range high?
3. What was the opening range low?
4. Did price break the range?
5. Did the break hold?
6. Was the trader late or emotional?

The opening range should create structure, not confusion.

## Example

A stock opens and trades between $2.00 and $2.20 during the first 5 minutes. That $2.00 to $2.20 area can be considered the opening range if the trader uses a 5-minute opening range.

If price later breaks above $2.20 and holds, traders may review it as an opening range breakout.

## How To Review Opening Range

A good opening range review looks at range quality and trade response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the range clearly defined?
2. Did the trader wait for the range to form?
3. Did price break above or below the range?
4. Did volume support the move?
5. Did the trader manage risk around the range?
6. Did the range remain useful later in the session?

The goal is to understand whether the opening range helped the trade plan.

## FAQ

### What is opening range?

Opening range is the high and low formed during a selected early window of the regular trading session.

### What time period is used for opening range?

It depends on the trader. Common windows include 1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or the first hour.

### Does opening range guarantee direction?

No. Opening range does not guarantee a breakout, breakdown, or trend.

### Why do traders watch opening range?

They watch it because it can define early support, resistance, breakout levels, breakdown levels, and trade structure.

### What is an opening range breakout?

An opening range breakout happens when price breaks above the opening range high.
