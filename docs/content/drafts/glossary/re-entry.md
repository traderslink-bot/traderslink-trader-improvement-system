# Re-Entry

Re-entry means entering a trade again after previously exiting it. Traders may re-enter when a setup returns, a level reclaims, or the original idea becomes valid again.

A re-entry is not automatically good or bad. It can be part of a disciplined plan, but it can also become revenge trading if the trader is trying to make back a loss emotionally.

## Simple Definition

Re-entry means taking a new position in a trade idea after exiting a prior position.

For example:

1. A trader buys a breakout.
2. The breakout fails and the trader exits.
3. Later, the stock reclaims the breakout level with volume.
4. The trader enters again.

That second entry is a re-entry.

## Why Re-Entry Matters

Re-entry matters because it can show discipline or emotion depending on why it happened.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may re-enter because the setup returned, or they may re-enter because they are frustrated about missing a move or taking a loss.

A useful review asks:

1. Why did the trader re-enter?
2. Was there a new valid setup?
3. Was risk defined again?
4. Did the trader chase after exiting?
5. Was the re-entry revenge trading?
6. Did the re-entry follow the plan?

A re-entry should be reviewed as a fresh trade decision.

## Example

A trader exits a failed breakout when the stock loses the breakout level. Later, the stock reclaims the level and holds with volume. The trader re-enters with a new stop and new plan.

That can be a structured re-entry.

If the trader re-enters immediately after a loss without a new setup, the behavior may be emotional.

## How To Review Re-Entries

A good re-entry review looks at setup quality and trader behavior.

Useful questions include:

1. What changed after the first exit?
2. Was the new entry based on a setup?
3. Was the new risk plan clear?
4. Was the trader trying to recover a loss?
5. Did the re-entry improve or worsen the day?
6. Has emotional re-entry happened before?

The goal is to separate disciplined second chances from impulse trades.

## FAQ

### What is re-entry in trading?

Re-entry means entering a trade again after previously exiting it.

### Is re-entry bad?

Not always. A re-entry can be valid if a fresh setup appears and risk is defined.

### When can re-entry become a problem?

It can become a problem when it is emotional, rushed, or done to recover a loss without a valid setup.

### Should re-entries have a new plan?

Yes. A re-entry should usually be treated as a fresh trade with a clear reason, entry, stop, and risk plan.

### How can traders review re-entries?

They can review the reason for re-entry, setup quality, risk plan, timing, and whether the trade was disciplined or emotional.
