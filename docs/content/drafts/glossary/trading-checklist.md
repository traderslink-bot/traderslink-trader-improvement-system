# Trading Checklist

A trading checklist is a list of conditions a trader reviews before taking or managing a trade. It helps the trader slow down, confirm the setup, define risk, and avoid impulsive decisions.

A trading checklist does not guarantee a trade will work. It helps make the decision process more consistent and reviewable.

## Simple Definition

A trading checklist is a pre-trade or in-trade review list.

A trading checklist may include:

1. Setup name.
2. Catalyst.
3. Key levels.
4. Entry area.
5. Stop or invalidation.
6. Position size.
7. Risk amount.
8. Volume and liquidity.
9. Exit plan.
10. Reason not to trade.

The checklist should match the trader’s strategy and rules.

## Why Trading Checklists Matter

Trading checklists matter because they reduce rushed decisions.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may know what to check but skip the steps when a stock is moving quickly.

A useful review asks:

1. Was the checklist used?
2. Which item failed?
3. Did the trader ignore a warning?
4. Was risk defined before entry?
5. Did the checklist prevent bad trades?
6. Should the checklist be updated?

A checklist should support discipline, not become a box-checking exercise.

## Example

A trader’s checklist requires a catalyst, high relative volume, a clean level, and a defined stop. If a stock is moving but has no clear stop, the checklist may tell the trader to wait.

That can help prevent a chase entry.

## How To Review A Trading Checklist

A good checklist review compares checklist items with actual trades.

Useful questions include:

1. Were checklist items specific?
2. Did the trader complete the checklist before entry?
3. Did skipped items lead to losses?
4. Did the checklist catch weak setups?
5. Were there too many checklist items?
6. Which items should be changed?

The goal is to make the checklist practical and useful in real time.

## FAQ

### What is a trading checklist?

A trading checklist is a list of conditions a trader reviews before taking or managing a trade.

### Why use a trading checklist?

A checklist helps traders confirm setup quality, risk, and trade plan before acting.

### Is a checklist the same as a trading plan?

No. A trading plan is the broader system. A checklist is a practical tool used to apply the plan.

### What should be on a trading checklist?

It may include setup, catalyst, levels, entry, stop, risk, liquidity, volume, and exit plan.

### Can a checklist prevent all bad trades?

No. It can reduce impulsive decisions, but it does not guarantee good outcomes.
