# Technical Trader

A technical trader uses charts, price action, levels, patterns, indicators, or market structure to make trading decisions. Technical traders often focus on support, resistance, trend, volume, breakouts, breakdowns, and setup confirmation.

Technical trading does not guarantee accurate predictions. Charts can help organize decisions, but price can still fail, fake out, or move against the plan.

## Simple Definition

A technical trader makes decisions using chart-based information.

Technical traders may focus on:

1. Support.
2. Resistance.
3. Breakouts.
4. Breakdowns.
5. Trendlines.
6. Volume.
7. Candlestick patterns.
8. Moving averages.
9. Market structure.
10. Risk levels.

The goal is to use chart structure to plan and review trades.

## Why Technical Trader Review Matters

Technical trader review matters because chart analysis can become subjective.

This is where technical traders can get into trouble. They may draw levels after the fact, force patterns, or ignore invalidation when the chart changes.

A useful review asks:

1. Was the level clear before entry?
2. Did the setup meet criteria?
3. Did volume confirm the move?
4. Did the trader respect invalidation?
5. Was the chart read objective or forced?
6. Did the technical setup repeat well?

Technical review should compare the original chart plan with actual behavior.

## Example

A trader marks resistance at $2.50 and plans a breakout trade only if price breaks and holds above the level with volume. If price rejects at $2.50, the technical thesis may fail.

The review should focus on whether the trader followed that chart plan.

## How Technical Traders Can Review Performance

A good technical trading review looks at level quality and setup consistency.

Useful questions include:

1. Were levels marked before the trade?
2. Did the setup match the strategy?
3. Did indicators help or distract?
4. Did the trader chase after confirmation?
5. Did the trader respect support and resistance?
6. Which technical setups worked best?

The goal is to make technical analysis more consistent and reviewable.

## FAQ

### What is a technical trader?

A technical trader uses charts, price action, levels, patterns, indicators, or market structure to make trading decisions.

### Is technical trading the same as chart trading?

They are closely related. Technical traders usually rely heavily on charts and price behavior.

### What do technical traders watch?

They may watch support, resistance, trend, volume, breakouts, breakdowns, indicators, and candlestick patterns.

### Does technical analysis guarantee price direction?

No. Technical analysis does not guarantee future movement.

### How should technical trades be reviewed?

They can be reviewed for level quality, setup consistency, volume, invalidation, entry timing, and trade management.

### How does Trader Intelligence relate to technical traders?

Trader Intelligence is being built to help technical traders review chart context, execution, risk, and repeated decision patterns.
