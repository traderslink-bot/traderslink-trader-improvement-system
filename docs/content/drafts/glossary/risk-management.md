# Risk Management

Risk management is the process of controlling potential losses before, during, and after a trade. Traders use risk management to define how much they are willing to risk, where the trade is invalid, and when to stop trading.

Risk management does not guarantee profitability. It helps traders limit damage when trades do not work.

## Simple Definition

Risk management means planning and controlling trade risk.

Risk management may include:

1. Position sizing.
2. Stop levels.
3. Daily risk limits.
4. Maximum loss rules.
5. Trade invalidation levels.
6. Avoiding oversized positions.
7. Managing open risk.
8. Reviewing repeated mistakes.

The goal is to survive losing trades and protect trading capital.

## Why Risk Management Matters

Risk management matters because losses are part of trading.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may focus only on the potential upside and ignore what happens if the trade fails.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the planned risk?
2. Was the position size appropriate?
3. Was the stop respected?
4. Did the trader exceed daily risk?
5. Did one trade damage the account?
6. What risk habit repeated?

Risk management should be reviewed as part of every trading process.

## Example

A trader plans to risk $100 on a trade and exits when the invalidation level breaks. Another trader plans to risk $100 but keeps adding after the setup fails and loses much more.

The difference is not just the trade idea. It is risk management.

## How To Review Risk Management

A good risk review looks at planned risk versus actual risk.

Useful questions include:

1. Was risk defined before entry?
2. Did the trader follow the stop?
3. Was size adjusted for volatility?
4. Did open risk change after adding?
5. Was the daily loss limit respected?
6. Did the trader recover discipline after a loss?

The goal is to identify whether risk rules were followed.

## FAQ

### What is risk management in trading?

Risk management is the process of defining and controlling potential losses.

### Why is risk management important?

It helps traders limit damage when trades fail or market conditions change.

### What are examples of risk management?

Examples include position sizing, stops, daily loss limits, max loss rules, and invalidation levels.

### Does risk management guarantee profit?

No. Risk management does not guarantee profit, but it can help control downside.

### How can traders review risk management?

They can compare planned risk with actual risk and look for repeated rule-breaking.
