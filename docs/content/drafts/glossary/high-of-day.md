# High Of Day

High of day is the highest price a stock reaches during the trading day. Traders often watch high of day because it can become a breakout level, momentum reference, rejection area, or target.

High of day does not guarantee continuation. A stock can break high of day and keep going, fake out, reject, or trade sideways.

## Simple Definition

High of day is the highest traded price during the current trading day.

Traders may use high of day as:

1. A breakout level.
2. A momentum reference.
3. A resistance area.
4. A target level.
5. A rejection area.
6. A scanner condition.
7. A trade management marker.
8. A level for review after the trade.

The meaning depends on volume, context, and how price behaves near the level.

## Why High Of Day Matters

High of day matters because many traders watch it as a sign of intraday strength.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may chase a high-of-day break after the move is already extended or ignore a quick rejection.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the high-of-day level?
2. Did price break above it?
3. Did volume support the break?
4. Did price hold the level?
5. Was the entry planned or late?
6. Did the trader exit if the break failed?

High of day should be reviewed with structure, not treated as a signal by itself.

## Example

A stock trades up to $2.50 during the morning and pulls back. Later, it returns to $2.50 and breaks above it.

That move may be a high-of-day breakout. If price immediately falls back under $2.50, it may become a fakeout or rejection.

## How To Review High Of Day

A good high-of-day review looks at level quality, volume, and execution.

Useful questions include:

1. Was high of day clear?
2. Was the stock already extended?
3. Did the trader wait for confirmation?
4. Did the entry happen near the level or far above it?
5. Did the breakout hold?
6. Did the trader manage risk after rejection?

The goal is to understand whether the high-of-day trade had structure or was a chase.

## FAQ

### What is high of day?

High of day is the highest price a stock reaches during the current trading day.

### Why do traders watch high of day?

They watch it because it can act as a breakout level, momentum reference, target, or rejection area.

### Does a high-of-day break guarantee continuation?

No. Price can break high of day and still fake out, reject, or fail.

### What is HOD trading?

HOD usually means high of day. HOD trading often refers to watching or trading around the high-of-day level.

### How should traders review high-of-day trades?
