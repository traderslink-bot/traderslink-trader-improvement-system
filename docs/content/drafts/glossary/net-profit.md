# Net Profit

Net profit is the amount left after gains, losses, and trading costs are accounted for. Traders use net profit to understand the final result of a trade, day, week, month, setup, or strategy.

Net profit is important, but it does not explain the full quality of the trading process by itself. A trader still needs to review risk, drawdown, execution, fees, and trade management.

## Simple Definition

Net profit is what remains after subtracting losses and costs from profits.

A simple version is:

1. Gross profit from winners.
2. Minus gross loss from losers.
3. Minus costs such as commissions, fees, or borrow costs when applicable.
4. Equals net profit.

Net profit shows the final result after the losing side and costs are considered.

## Why Net Profit Matters

Net profit matters because it shows whether the trading results were positive after losses and costs.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may focus on big winners or gross profit while ignoring losses, fees, slippage, or one oversized mistake.

A useful review asks:

1. What was the net profit?
2. What was gross profit?
3. What was gross loss?
4. Were fees or slippage meaningful?
5. Did one trade create most of the result?
6. Did the net profit come from repeatable process?

Net profit is the result, not the full explanation.

## Example

A trader has $2,000 in gross profit and $1,300 in gross loss. Before fees, the net profit is $700. If fees and costs are $100, the final net profit is $600.

That number is useful, but the trader still needs to review how it was produced.

## How To Review Net Profit

A good net profit review looks at the quality behind the result.

Useful questions include:

1. Which setups produced the net profit?
2. Were profits repeatable or from one outlier trade?
3. Were losses controlled?
4. Did fees reduce results?
5. Did execution quality support the result?
6. Did the trader follow the plan?

The goal is to understand what created the final result.

## FAQ

### What is net profit in trading?

Net profit is the final result after subtracting losses and trading costs from profits.

### Is net profit the same as gross profit?

No. Gross profit only counts winning trades. Net profit accounts for losses and costs.

### Why does net profit matter?

It shows the actual result after the winning and losing sides of trading are combined.

### Can net profit hide bad trading habits?

Yes. A trader can have positive net profit while still making risky or inconsistent decisions.

### Should traders review net profit by setup?

Yes. Reviewing net profit by setup can help identify which strategies are helping or hurting results.
