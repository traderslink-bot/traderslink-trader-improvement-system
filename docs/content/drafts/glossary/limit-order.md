# Limit Order

A limit order is an order to buy or sell only at a specified price or better. Traders use limit orders when price control matters more than immediate execution.

A limit order can help control slippage, but it does not guarantee a fill. If price does not reach the limit or there is not enough liquidity, the order may only partially fill or may not fill at all.

## Simple Definition

A limit order sets the price a trader is willing to accept.

A buy limit order sets the highest price the trader is willing to pay. A sell limit order sets the lowest price the trader is willing to accept.

For example:

1. Buy limit: $2.00.
2. The order only fills at $2.00 or lower.
3. Sell limit: $2.20.
4. The order only fills at $2.20 or higher.

The benefit is price control. The tradeoff is possible missed fills.

## Why Limit Orders Matter

Limit orders matter because they help traders avoid paying far more or selling far lower than planned.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may use a limit order but then chase the price higher after missing the fill, turning a controlled entry into an emotional one.

A useful review asks:

1. Why was a limit order used?
2. Was the limit price realistic?
3. Did the order fill fully or partially?
4. Was the missed fill acceptable?
5. Did the trader chase after missing the fill?
6. Did the limit order improve execution quality?

Limit orders should be reviewed with trade plan and liquidity context.

## Example

A trader wants to buy a pullback at $2.00 and places a buy limit order at $2.00. Price only drops to $2.03 and then moves higher, so the order does not fill.

The trader should review whether missing the fill was acceptable or whether they changed the plan emotionally afterward.

## How To Review Limit Orders

A good limit order review looks at price control and opportunity cost.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the limit price based on structure?
2. Did the trader avoid bad slippage?
3. Did the trader miss the trade?
4. Did the trader chase after a missed fill?
5. Did the order partially fill?
6. Was liquidity strong enough at the limit price?

The goal is to understand whether the order type matched the trade situation.

## FAQ

### What is a limit order?

A limit order is an order to buy or sell at a specified price or better.

### Does a limit order guarantee a fill?

No. A limit order controls price, but it may not fill if price does not reach the limit or liquidity is not available.

### What is a buy limit order?

A buy limit order sets the highest price a trader is willing to pay.

### What is a sell limit order?

A sell limit order sets the lowest price a trader is willing to accept.

### Can limit orders reduce slippage?

They can help control price, but they may also miss fills or only partially fill.
