# Replay Trading

Replay trading is a practice method where traders replay historical market action as if it were happening live. It can help traders practice setups, entries, exits, risk rules, and decision-making without waiting for the next live market session.

Replay trading does not perfectly match live trading. Real-time pressure, live fills, liquidity, slippage, and real money emotions can still be different.

## Simple Definition

Replay trading means practicing with historical market action in a replay environment.

Replay trading may be used to practice:

1. Chart reading.
2. Setup recognition.
3. Entry timing.
4. Exit timing.
5. Stop placement.
6. Risk management.
7. Trade journaling.
8. Replay review.
9. Market structure decisions.
10. Discipline under simulated pressure.

The goal is to build decision skills through repetition.

## Why Replay Trading Matters

Replay trading matters because it lets traders practice many market scenarios faster than waiting for live setups.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may speed through replay too quickly, use hindsight, or take unrealistic trades because no real money is involved.

A useful review asks:

1. Was the replay treated seriously?
2. Were trades taken without hindsight?
3. Were entries and exits realistic?
4. Were risk rules followed?
5. Were trades journaled?
6. Did the practice reveal repeated mistakes?

Replay trading should be reviewed like any other practice session.

## Example

A trader replays a previous high-volume gap-up day and practices breakout entries. They pause the replay before each decision and write down the planned entry, stop, and reason for the trade.

That makes the replay more useful than simply watching what happened.

## How To Review Replay Trading

A good replay review looks at discipline and realism.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the market replay run at a realistic speed?
2. Did the trader avoid hindsight bias?
3. Were trades logged as they happened?
4. Did the trader follow the setup checklist?
5. Did execution assumptions make sense?
6. What pattern repeated across replay sessions?

The goal is to improve decision quality before live trading.

## FAQ

### What is replay trading?

Replay trading is practicing with historical market action as if it were happening live.

### Is replay trading the same as backtesting?

Not exactly. Backtesting usually tests rules on historical data. Replay trading usually practices decision-making through a simulated market replay.

### Is replay trading useful for beginners?

It can be useful because beginners can practice many scenarios without risking real money.

### Can replay trading create bad habits?

Yes. If traders use hindsight, skip risk rules, or trade unrealistically, replay practice can create poor habits.

### Should replay trades be journaled?

Yes. Journaling replay trades makes the practice easier to review and compare over time.
