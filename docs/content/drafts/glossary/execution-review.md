# Execution Review

Execution review is the process of analyzing how a trade was entered, managed, and exited. It focuses on the trader’s actual decisions, fills, timing, order behavior, and trade management.

Execution review is different from simply checking profit or loss. A trade can make money and still have poor execution. A trade can lose money and still have disciplined execution.

## Simple Definition

Execution review means reviewing how the trader handled the trade.

An execution review may include:

1. Entry timing.
2. Entry price.
3. Fill price.
4. Order type.
5. Slippage.
6. Position size.
7. Adds and reductions.
8. Exit timing.
9. Exit price.
10. Trade management decisions.

The goal is to review the process behind the result.

## Why Execution Review Matters

Execution review matters because repeated execution mistakes can damage performance even when trade ideas are reasonable.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may blame the setup when the bigger issue was chasing, poor sizing, late exits, or ignoring stops.

A useful review asks:

1. Was the entry planned?
2. Was the fill acceptable?
3. Was risk defined?
4. Was the exit disciplined?
5. Did the trader chase or hesitate?
6. Did execution improve or weaken the trade?

Execution review helps separate idea quality from trade handling.

## Example

A trader identifies a strong breakout setup but enters far above the breakout level after the stock is extended. The trade idea may have been valid, but the execution review may show a late entry and weaker risk-reward.

That distinction matters.

## How To Review Execution

A good execution review looks at decision points in order.

Useful questions include:

1. What was the planned entry?
2. What was the actual entry?
3. Did the trader follow the risk plan?
4. Did order type affect the fill?
5. Were exits planned or emotional?
6. What execution habit repeated?

The goal is to identify behaviors that can be improved.

## FAQ

### What is execution review?

Execution review is the process of analyzing how a trade was entered, managed, and exited.

### Is execution review the same as trade review?

Execution review is part of trade review. It focuses specifically on the trader’s actions and fills.

### What should traders review in execution?

They can review entry, exit, order type, fill price, slippage, sizing, adds, reductions, and discipline.

### Can a winning trade have bad execution?

Yes. A winning trade can still include late entries, poor risk, or emotional exits.

### Why does execution review matter?

It helps traders identify repeated process mistakes that profit and loss alone may hide.

### How does Trader Intelligence relate to execution review?

Trader Intelligence is being built to help traders review execution, entries, exits, fills, and repeated decision patterns.
