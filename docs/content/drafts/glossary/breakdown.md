# Breakdown

A breakdown happens when price moves below a key support level, range, pattern, or prior low. Traders often watch breakdowns because they can signal weakness, selling pressure, or a failed support area.

A breakdown does not guarantee continued downside. Price can reclaim the level, reverse, or trap traders who react too late.

## Simple Definition

A breakdown is a move below an important support level.

A breakdown may happen below:

1. Support.
2. Low of day.
3. Previous day low.
4. Chart pattern support.
5. Consolidation range.
6. Moving average.
7. Psychological price level.
8. News reaction support.

The level should be meaningful enough to review after the trade.

## Why Breakdowns Matter

Breakdowns matter because they can show that buyers failed to defend a level.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may hold a long position after support breaks, add emotionally, or ignore that the original thesis has failed.

A useful review asks:

1. What level broke?
2. Was the level important?
3. Did volume confirm weakness?
4. Did price reclaim the level quickly?
5. Did the trader exit when support failed?
6. Did the trader hold after invalidation?

A breakdown can be a signal to review risk, not a guarantee of direction.

## Example

A stock holds $2.00 support for several hours. Price then breaks below $2.00 with increasing selling and cannot reclaim the level.

That can be a breakdown. If price quickly reclaims $2.00, the breakdown may have failed.

## How To Review A Breakdown

A good breakdown review looks at both price behavior and trade response.

Useful questions include:

1. Was the support level clear?
2. Did the trader have an invalidation plan?
3. Did price confirm below support?
4. Did the trader exit or reduce risk?
5. Did the trader add after support failed?
6. Did the breakdown turn into a reclaim?

The goal is to understand whether the trader responded to weakness according to plan.

## FAQ

### What is a breakdown?

A breakdown is a move below a key support level, range, pattern, or prior low.

### Does a breakdown guarantee more downside?

No. A breakdown can fail, reclaim, or reverse.

### What confirms a breakdown?

Confirmation may include volume, price holding below support, failed reclaim attempts, or continued selling pressure.

### What is a failed breakdown?

A failed breakdown happens when price breaks below support but quickly reclaims the level.

### Why do traders review breakdowns?

Breakdowns can show whether a trade thesis failed and whether the trader managed risk properly.

Trader Intelligence is being built to help traders review breakdown behavior, execution, risk, and trade management patterns.
