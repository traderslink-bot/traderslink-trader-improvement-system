# Performance Review

Performance review is the process of analyzing trading results across a group of trades. Traders use performance review to understand win rate, profit factor, drawdown, average win, average loss, setup quality, and repeated behavior patterns.

Performance review does not guarantee improvement. It gives traders information they can use to make better review decisions and adjust their process.

## Simple Definition

Performance review means studying trading results over a sample of trades.

A performance review may include:

1. Net profit.
2. Win rate.
3. Average win.
4. Average loss.
5. Profit factor.
6. Drawdown.
7. Setup results.
8. Time-of-day results.
9. Risk behavior.
10. Execution patterns.

The goal is to understand what is helping or hurting performance.

## Why Performance Review Matters

Performance review matters because one trade does not tell the full story.

This is where traders can get into trouble. They may overreact to one big win or loss instead of reviewing enough trades to see real patterns.

A useful review asks:

1. What setups performed best?
2. What setups performed worst?
3. Was win rate enough for the strategy?
4. Were losses larger than wins?
5. Was drawdown controlled?
6. What behavior repeated across trades?

Performance review helps traders review the bigger picture.

## Example

A trader reviews 100 trades and finds that overall P&L is flat. After sorting by setup, the trader sees that one setup is profitable while another setup creates most losses.

That kind of review can help the trader focus on what is working and reduce what is not.

## How To Review Performance

A good performance review looks at data quality and meaningful groups.

Useful questions include:

1. Is the sample size large enough?
2. Are trades tagged consistently?
3. Which setups are strongest?
4. Which mistakes repeat?
5. Is performance improving over time?
6. What rule should change next?

The goal is to turn trading data into practical feedback.

## FAQ

### What is performance review in trading?

Performance review is the process of analyzing trading results across a group of trades.

### How is performance review different from trade review?

Trade review often focuses on individual trades. Performance review looks at broader results across many trades.

### What metrics are used in performance review?

Metrics may include win rate, profit factor, average win, average loss, drawdown, net profit, and setup performance.

### Why does sample size matter?

A small sample can be misleading. More trades can make patterns easier to review.

### Can performance review reveal behavior problems?

Yes. It can show repeated mistakes such as chasing, overtrading, holding losers, or cutting winners too early.
