# TradersLink SEO Content Plan Starting Point

## Purpose

Use this as the starting brief for ChatGPT or any content writer building the
TradersLink website content plan. The goal is to create SEO pages and drafts
that accurately describe the current product direction without making claims the
app cannot support yet.

TradersLink should be positioned as a trading tools platform. Trader
Intelligence is one major tool inside that platform, not the whole company.

## Product Positioning

TradersLink is a beta suite of trading tools for active small-cap and day
traders. The platform combines:

- Small-cap scanner alerts in Discord.
- AI summaries for small-cap press releases and SEC filings.
- Market scanner filtering for news and filings that may matter to active day
  traders.
- Auto-generated support and resistance chart levels attached to scanner and
  press-release stock cards.
- Stock context such as float, market cap, short interest, and related trading
  information.
- An AI chart-following system, coming soon, that uses market data and candles
  to track breaks, weakening setups, and possible dip areas as charts develop.
- Trader Intelligence, coming soon, for broker execution imports, trade review,
  execution analysis, mistake tracking, performance insights, session timing,
  and evidence-backed coaching.

Important positioning rule: do not make it sound like TradersLink is a live
human trade-calling room. It is software, AI, market-data tooling, scanner
logic, execution analysis, and trader review intelligence.

## Recommended Site Architecture

- `/`  
  TradersLink homepage. Primary message: trading tools platform for scanner
  alerts, AI news and filing summaries, generated chart levels, and Trader
  Intelligence coming soon.

- `/beta/`  
  $30 Discord beta offer page. Explain what is included now and what is coming

- `/scanner/`  
  Small-cap scanner page. Focus on live movement detection, filtering, Discord
  alerts, and small-cap trader workflow.

  Press Release App v2 page. Focus on AI summaries for press releases and SEC
  filings, scanner filtering, trading-card context, and Discord alerts.

- `/chart-levels/`  
  Generated chart levels page. Focus on auto-generated support and resistance
  levels attached to scanner and news cards.

- `/ai-chart-following/`  
  Coming-soon page for AI chart-following. Focus on chart/candle monitoring,
  level breaks, weakening setups, and possible dip areas. Keep this clearly
  marked as coming soon.

### Trader Intelligence Money Pages

- `/trader-intelligence/`  
  Main page for the Trader Intelligence app. Position as the coming execution
  review and trader improvement system.

- `/trading-journal-app/`  
  SEO money page for "trading journal app" and "day trading journal." Explain
  how the product goes beyond manual notes by importing broker executions and
  turning them into review data.

- `/features/trade-review/`  
  Focus on per-trade review, saved review queues, checklist behavior, decision
  review, and evidence-backed notes.

- `/features/execution-analysis/`  
  Focus on entries, exits, adds, reductions, holding behavior, gross P/L,
  session timing, and trade management feedback.

- `/features/trading-mistake-tracker/`  
  Focus on mistake detection, repeated behavior, adverse adds, chasing, open
  trades, rule candidates, and improvement tracking.

- `/features/support-resistance-trade-review/`  
  Focus on support and resistance based review. Explain that market context and
  chart levels come from the levels system, while Trader Intelligence uses that
  context for review when available.

- `/features/session-time-analysis/`  
  Focus on premarket, market open, midday, post-market, overnight, and hourly
  performance insights.

- `/features/broker-execution-import/`  
  Focus on importing executions from broker CSVs, including generic mapped CSV
  support, duplicate detection, repair flow, and saved analytics.

## Schema Suggestions

Use only when the page content supports it:

- `SoftwareApplication` for product pages.
- `FAQPage` for pages with real FAQ sections.
- `Article` or `BlogPosting` for blog posts.
- `Organization` for the homepage or about page.
- `Offer` for the beta page, with $30 USD beta access language.

Do not use review/rating schema unless real reviews exist.

## Notes For ChatGPT Content Drafting

When drafting, separate platform copy from Trader Intelligence copy:

- Platform copy should cover scanner, press releases, SEC filings, chart levels,
  Discord alerts, beta access, and coming tools.
- Trader Intelligence copy should cover the trading journal app, trade review
  tool, execution analysis, mistake tracking, performance insights, session
  timing, and support/resistance based review.

Keep the writing plain and direct. The audience is active traders who want tools
that help them move faster and review better. Avoid hype. Make the beta status
clear, and always avoid financial-advice language.
